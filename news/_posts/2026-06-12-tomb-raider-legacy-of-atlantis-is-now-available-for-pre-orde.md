On February 12, 2027, Crystal Dynamics and Amazon Games will release Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis across consoles and PC. The game is a remake of the original Tomb Raider video game from 1996.

Rather than being a shot-for-shot remake, Legacy of Atlantis builds upon the original story with expanded content and is described as “honoring the spirit and atmosphere of Lara Croft's debut adventure.”

Ahead of the game’s release, physical versions for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, and Nintendo Switch 2 are now available to pre-order at Walmart. The Standard Edition is priced at $59.99.

A Deluxe Edition is also available for $69.99. It includes an early unlock of Lara’s Parisian Fugitive Outfit and access to a post-launch DLC story pack.

Those who pre-order either edition of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will receive an early unlock of Lara’s Survivor Outfit. Pre-orders for the Deluxe Edition will also come with 48 hours of early access to the game.
