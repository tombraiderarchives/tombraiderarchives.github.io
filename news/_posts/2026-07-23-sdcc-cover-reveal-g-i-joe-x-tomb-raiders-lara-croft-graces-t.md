The Hasbro team just wrapped its annual G.I. Joe panel at Comic-Con International: San Diego (SDCC), formally serving up the first look at TOMB RAIDER’s Lara Croft as part of The Classified Series. With that, The Toy Book can officially reveal the editorial cover of its 2026 Action, Adventure, & Mystery Issue.

Shot by Hasbro photographer Matthew Cohen, @shootingthegalaxy on Instagram, this epic cover features Croft — as a 6-inch scale action figure — heroically using The Toy Book masthead for support as she hunts for relics on Cobra Island. The figure from Hasbro and Crystal Dynamics celebrates 30 years of TOMB RAIDER and comes with dual pistols, the Dagger of Xian, plus 13 more accessories.

Our latest issue is hitting subscriber mailboxes now, with a digital street date set for July 31. The Action, Adventure, & Mystery Issue is packed with exclusive features, new product previews, and updates you won’t find anywhere else.

The G.I. Joe Classified Series Retro Cardback Collaborative Lara Croft TOMB RAIDER will be available to pre-order at 5 p.m. ET today at Hasbro Pulse. Slated for delivery in November, the figure is designed for adventurers ages 4 and up, and carries a $44.99 MSRP.
