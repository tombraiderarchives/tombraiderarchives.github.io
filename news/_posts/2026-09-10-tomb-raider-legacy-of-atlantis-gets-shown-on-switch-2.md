Nintendo has posted several images of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis running on Switch 2 this week. The remake will be released on the Japanese manufacturer’s console, as well as on other platforms, on February 12, 2027.

In addition to the trailer released during Nintendo Direct, the Japanese company has opened pre-orders on the eShop. The standard edition of the game is priced at €59.99, and the Deluxe Edition at €69.99 (which includes 48 hours of early access).
