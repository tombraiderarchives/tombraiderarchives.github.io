Anime fans are waiting to watch Tomb Raider King Episode 11 as this series might be taking a serious turn. With Joo-Heon facing new problems for which he does not have an answer, the main lead cannot give up. With Irene's physical condition deteriorating rapidly and Joo-Heon going on a quest to find the Xufu's Relic, the upcoming episode might continue maintaining the intensity of the story, which has been teased in this week's installment.

According to official sources, it has been confirmed that Tomb Raider King Episode 11 is going to be released on Wednesday, September 16, 2026. This series is following a weekly release schedule, and all the latest installments will first air on a local Japanese broadcast channel. In this week's episode, we see how Joo-Heon and Irene had to deal with the Midas Relic. She had to use her abilities but did not have much confidence in herself. That is when Joo-Heon encouraged her, which led her to establish a complete contract with the relic.

But using her powers had some consequences because there was a side effect. Irene ended up developing an intense desire to smell Joo-Heon's scent. It is also revealed that her parents were suffering from Relic Syndrome, and our main lead realises that there is something unusual about how rapidly the illness has been spreading.

In the end, he decided that it would be best to look for a medical relic that might be helpful in creating a cure. This led him to investigate the Xufu's relic. Eventually, he found himself underground, where Joo-Heon encountered Edward.

After looking at the ending of the recent installment, here is what fans could expect from Tomb Raider King Episode 11:

Joo-Heon's search for Xufu's relic: The biggest storyline in Tomb Raider King Episode 11 will likely be Joo-Heon's hunt for Xufu's Relic. Since the relic could be connected to the creation of medicine, obtaining it may become one of his most important missions yet. This could put Joo-Heon directly on a collision course with another powerful relic user.

Keira's mysterious plan: Keira is becoming increasingly dangerous, especially after demonstrating the devastating ability of her Sekhmet Relic. Interestingly, the backlash from using the relic temporarily turns her into a child, something fans found both hilarious and surprisingly adorable. Tomb Raider King Episode 11 could reveal more about what she is searching for and why she is keeping her activities hidden from Pandora and other organizations.

Irene's relic side effects continue: Fans can also expect more comedic moments involving Irene. Her Midas Relic has amplified her desire to stay close to Joo-Heon, and her obsession with his scent has already become one of the funniest running jokes. However, the side effect could become more complicated if Irene uses the relic again in Tomb Raider King Episode 11.

In the end, Tomb Raider King Episode 11 has plenty of storylines to develop, from Joo-Heon's search for Xufu's Relic to the mystery surrounding Irene's parents and Keira's secret plans. The episode could also provide more information about Relic Syndrome and whether a cure can actually be created.
