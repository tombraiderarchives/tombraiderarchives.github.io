Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Dogs have released the eighth episode of their video series dedicated to the development of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis. This remake of the first game will be released on PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC next February 12, 2027.

In this new developer diary, we get to catch a glimpse of the environments of the game’s second level, which takes place in Greece. Of course, it has been modernized for the occasion. A few never-before-seen clips from the game are hidden within this promotional video.
