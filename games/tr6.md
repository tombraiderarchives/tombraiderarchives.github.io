The final Core Design Tomb Raider and the most troubled entry in the franchise. Angel of Darkness attempted a dramatic shift in tone — a darker, grittier story set in Paris and Prague, with Lara framed for the murder of her mentor Werner Von Croy and hunted by police. A new male playable character, Kurtis Trent, was introduced partway through.

Released unfinished after a troubled development cycle, the game was plagued by bugs and poor performance on PlayStation 2. Its critical and commercial disappointment led Eidos Interactive to hand the series to Crystal Dynamics, ending Core Design's decade-long stewardship.

Despite its flaws, Angel of Darkness has a cult following that appreciates its ambitious, atmospheric tone.
