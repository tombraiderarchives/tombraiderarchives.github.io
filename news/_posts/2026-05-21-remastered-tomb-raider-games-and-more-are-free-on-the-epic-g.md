The Epic Games Store kicked off mystery giveaways last week, and it's continuing the trend for at least a couple of more weeks. The latest drop was revealed to be another double giveaway, replacing last week's Telltale Batman games and Sunderfolk options. This time, you can grab the Tomb Raider I-III Remastered Starring Lara Croft bundle as well as a copy of Down in Bermuda.

The Tomb Raider bundle lands from Aspyr, bringing fans of the 90s classics a complete version of the first three games with expansion packs and secret levels. These Lara Croft adventures have other upgrades too. The games feature a visual toggle to switch between classic and modernized graphics, control options to play with full camera movement, a new challenge mode, and more features.

Next, Down in Bermuda tells the tale of "an adventurous aviator" that's stranded in the Bermuda Triangle. Developed by Yak & Co., the game has players solving puzzles, collecting magic orbs, and cracking codes to escape the six unique islands it offers before our traveler can go home.

"Down in Bermuda is a quirky adventure filled with puzzles to solve and mysteries to uncover," says the studio, explaining the casual title. "Our adventurous aviator Milton finds himself trapped in a time bubble deep inside the Bermuda and needs your help to escape."

The newly available Tomb Raider I-III Remastered Starring Lara Croft bundle and the Down in Bermuda giveaways on the Epic Games Store are set to run until May 28, giving PC gamers seven days to claim the latest offer as usual. Once this promotion comes to an end, a new freebie will take its place on the same day, which remains a mystery for now.

Don't forget that mobile gamers can grab the Epic Game Store's refreshed giveaway on the Android and iOS stores this week as well, which happens to be a copy of Monument Valley 3.
