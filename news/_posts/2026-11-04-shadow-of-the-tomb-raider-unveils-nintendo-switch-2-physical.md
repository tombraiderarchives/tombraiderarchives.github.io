Shadow of the Tomb Raider, which recently received a shadowdrop on Nintendo Switch 2, is now confirmed for physical release.

Limited Run Games will offer a standard copy and a collector’s edition. With both, you’ll get the full game on the cartridge. The collector’s edition adds a steelcase, Trinity patch, and a light up Divine Source replica desk ornament.

Our fierce explorer is back to encounter the unknown. Embark on Lara’s most treacherous journey through Siberia to the lost city of Kitezh and unlock the mysteries of the past.

An Exhilarating Story — Outwit the insidious secret society, Trinity, and their paramilitary operatives as you race to uncover the Divine Source, said to harness the power of immortality, hidden deep in the Siberian wilderness.

Challenging Puzzle Tombs — Follow in the footsteps of those before you—the legendary Prophet, the ancient Remnant civilization, and Trinity—to outwit environmental puzzles and ancient traps guarding the relics within.

Dynamic Combat — Prove you’re worthy of the Croft legacy as you outmaneuver enemies using scavenged supplies and Lara’s versatile arsenal. Engage in combat or utilize stealth to silently eliminate Trinity soldiers.

Survival Upgrades & Customization — Gather resources to craft essential gear and upgrade Lara’s skills. Discover weapon parts and ancient texts that will help you create the specialized ammunition Lara needs to survive against the relentless forces of nature and of Trinity.

The expedition continues! All DLC and exclusive content are included in this complete edition:

All Season Pass Content & DLCs: Baba Yaga: The Temple of the Witch, Cold Darkness Awakened, Blood

All Extras: 35 Expansion Cards, Extreme Survivor Difficulty, 5 Classic Lara Skins, and TR III Reimagined “Antarctica” Outfit

You can pre-order the Shadow of the Tomb Raider Nintendo Switch 2 physical release starting November 6, 2026 here. We also have information about the Rise of the Tomb Raider Nintendo Switch 2 physical release.
