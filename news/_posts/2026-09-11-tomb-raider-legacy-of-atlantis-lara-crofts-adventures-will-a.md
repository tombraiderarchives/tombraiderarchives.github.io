Lara Croft is hitting the road on another platform... Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, a remake of the iconic early adventures in the series, arrives on February 12, 2027 for PC, PS5, Xbox Series... and Nintendo Switch 2! The newsBreaking release for the Big N console came during a Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026. Developed by Flying Wild Hog, in collaboration with Crystal Dynamics, and published by Amazon Games, this remake of the original 1996 Tomb Raider seeks to revisit the adventurer’s early days with a modernized approach that blends exploration, puzzles and action.

Designed to appeal to both longtime fans and newcomers alike, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis stays true to the series' core spirit, blending exploration, intricate puzzles, verticality, iconic acrobatics, and dynamic combat. Built entirely on Unreal Engine 5, the game promises sprawling, highly detailed environments alongside a more modernized presentation. During a recent Q&A session, the developers revealed plans for a complete overhaul of the puzzles, new areas to explore, and the reimagining of classic sequences—such as the encounter with the T-Rex, now transformed into a more spectacular action scene.

"We’ve completely rebuilt Legacy of Atlantis using Unreal Engine 5, redesigned the puzzles, and added new areas. We also reimagined iconic moments, like the T-rex fight, transforming it into an epic action scene that fans will feel like they’re experiencing for the first time—while also delighting newcomers," say the Crystal Dynamics team. Their goal is to recapture the wonder of the franchise’s early days, all while delivering an experience that meets modern standards. This remake also fits into a unified timeline for the series, with the trilogy released between 2013 and 2018 now recognized as Lara Croft’s original story, set prior to the events revisited in Legacy of Atlantis.

"Some legends are meant to be revisited. Step into the shoes of Lara Croft as she ventures into exotic, ancient locales — from the lush jungles of Peru and the timeless ruins of Greece to the deserts of Egypt and a mysterious Mediterranean island shrouded in myth. Navigate treacherous terrain, outsmart deadly traps, and confront formidable predators in pursuit of scattered fragments of the Scion, an artifact with unimaginable power."

Coinciding with the Game Awards, it was also announced that Alix Wilton Regan will lend her voice and motion capture talents to Lara Croft in Legacy of Atlantis. The actress, known for her roles in Mass Effect 3, Dragon Age: Inquisition, and Cyberpunk 2077, now brings Lara to life in this new chapter of the franchise, ensuring consistency with upcoming projects. The character’s movements have been extensively reworked to improve fluidity and accuracy, with a particular emphasis on verticality, stealth, and environmental interaction to solve puzzles.

Finally, players eager to customize their experience from day one will have access to an exclusive outfit: the Mediterranean suit. This skin can be unlocked by linking a Amazon Games account with either their PlayStation Network or Xbox Live ID. The game is already available for wishlist on digital storefronts, so fans can stay updated on the latest news ahead of the highly anticipated remake.

With Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog aim to deliver a fresh, modern take on a cornerstone of video gaming. They’re also shaping it to fit seamlessly into the future of Lara Croft. Whether you’re discovering the saga for the first time or returning out of nostalgia, this new chapter promises to be a must-play for fans of the franchise.
