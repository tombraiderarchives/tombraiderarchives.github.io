Amazon Game Studios and Crystal Dynamics have just released the seventh in a series of mini-documentaries on the making of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis.

This episode focuses on Lara Croft’s nemesis Jacqueline Natla, the immortal former ruler of Ancient Atlantis and CEO of Natla Technologies, who will oppose the eponymous Tomb Raider as she discovers the secrets of Atlantis.

Natla will be brought to life by Adriene Wilkinson, who breaks down what makes her such an iconic villain in Legacy of Atlantis.

“She might be a little terrifying - but oh will you love every second around her,” Wilkinson said. “Stories like Tomb Raider need someone like Natla because stories need the idea of a ‘noble foe.’ It happens within everyone’s life. You have someone within your life that motivates you to push harder, to go further, and Natla does that. She raises the stakes every time she does something.”
