The Game Awards 2025 feel like a distant memory for most of us at this point, with Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 emerging as the big winner on a record breaking night. The other big winners at the time were Tomb Raider fans, who got confirmation that two games were on the way in relatively short order.

The first was the next major entry in the series, Tomb Raider: Catalyst, the 14th entry in the series, and one that looks like an exciting next step. The other was Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, a ground-up remake of the original 1996 debut that, while yet to launch, is already offering players the chance to pick-up in-game goodies for when it does.

As first reported by PlayStation Lifestyle, publisher Amazon Games has confirmed that anyone who signs up for an Amazon Games iD account, and then subsequently links it to either their PlayStation Network, Steam ID, or Xbox account will get the exclusive Mediterranean Wetsuit Outfit pictured above.
