A heap of new content packed into Season 11 of The Crew Motorfest awaits!

New Playlists, a lineup of 20 new vehicles, 2 new islands to explore, new special events, stories to discover, winches to try out, wildlife to spot, all waiting to be claimed.

What else? Discover a packed playlist in collaboration with Crystal Dynamics with the new Tomb Raider themed DLC Playlist “Secrets of Ni’ihau” dropping alongside Season 11.

If that sounds interesting to you, then we can’t wait to show you some of what’s coming this season, so keep reading to find out more!

⁃ New Free Playlist: Kaua’i Expeditions ⁃ New DLC Pack in Collaboration: Tomb Raider Secrets of Ni’ihau ⁃ New Island(s): 2 New Islands Kaua’i (Free) & Ni’ihau (DLC Pack) are waiting to be discovered and explored! ⁃ New Vehicles: A lineup of 20 new vehicles including new planes and bikes will be added throughout Season 11 ⁃ Nintendo Switch 2 Drop: Coming October 8th. Dive into The Crew Motorfest and enjoy the world of Hawaii on a brand-new platform ⁃ New Wildlife: From Horses (YES!!) to Deer and Birds, 40+ Species of wildlife await to be found ⁃ New TrackForge kit: ‘Pure Dirt’ takes you on an off-road adventure ⁃ New Winch Tool Gameplay Feature ⁃ 1 New Story & 2 New Special Events ⁃ 14 Weeks of Main Stage

Dropping October 8th, dive into The Crew Motorfest wherever you are and discover or rediscover all the roads of the islands of Hawaii on a brand-new platform.

With 95 KM of asphalt and 105 km off-road for you to explore, this new Island north of O’ahu is waiting for you to explore it!

With the launch of Season 11, hit the road with 20 new vehicles including the stunning Bugatti Tourbillon. Discover off roads with the Lamborghini Huracán Steratto or with the Hyundai Crater concept.

Unmarked on any track list, waiting to be claimed. No stages, no cameras, no roaring crowds. Just you and the raw terrain, hidden roads and a silence waiting to be broken.

With the new gameplay feature the “Road Progression” explore or re-explore every unknown corner from O’ahu, Maui and Kaua’i.
