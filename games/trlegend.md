The narrative opens with a life changing plane crash in the Himalayas, shown as a flashback at the beginning. Lara is a young girl when the crash occurs, and her mother disappears under mysterious circumstances.

Years later, Lara searches for the scattered fragments of Excalibur, believing the sword may reveal the truth about her mother's fate. Along the way she encounters Amanda Evert, a former friend thought to be dead. Amanda is pursuing the sword for her own unknown reasons.

Their conflict draws Lara into a story shaped by myth, loss, and ancient power.
