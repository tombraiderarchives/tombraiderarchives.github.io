Tomb Raider: Catalyst may have been delayed from 2027 to 2028, according to comments from Amazon Games chief Jeffrey Gattis.

Speaking during an interview with The Game Business, Gattis discussed Amazon’s upcoming portfolio and seemingly revealed the revised launch window.

“We’ve obviously announced the two Tomb Raider titles: Atlantis comes 12th February next year and then we’ll follow on in 2028 with the next one,” Gattis said in the interview.

Tomb Raider: Catalyst was announced in December 2025 with a 2027 release window for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. The new adventure will take Lara Croft to northern India, but few gameplay or story details have been revealed.

Meanwhile, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, a ground-up reimagining of Lara’s original adventure, is scheduled for February 2027 after previously targeting 2026.
