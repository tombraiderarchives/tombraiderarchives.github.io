Steam’s Tomb Raider Adventure Bundle consists of nine titles for $15.70, compared with a combined $112.46 if purchased separately. This amounts to an 87% reduction and offers gamers an inexpensive opportunity to dive into an action-adventure series that, spans several generations. The bundle includes:

Tomb Raider I-III Remastered: Tomb Raider, Tomb Raider II and Tomb Raider III

Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered: Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation, Tomb Raider: Chronicles and Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness.

The last three titles, collectively known as the Survivor Trilogy, arrive in complete editions together with all previous DLC, while the older, remastered games (dating back to 1996) also include their expansions. The remasters offer sharper visuals and updated control options, while the Survivor Trilogy offers a more modern experience.

The sale coincides with the series’ 30th anniversary and will run until November 6, 2026, making it an affordable way to explore Lara Croft's history ahead of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, scheduled for launch on February 12, 2027.
