  <h1>Unified Timeline</h1>
  <p>A fan-constructed reading of the entire franchise as one cohesive continuity — merging the Classic, Legend, and Survivor games into a single story. Ordered by when events occur in Lara's life, not by release date. Not every narrative detail aligns perfectly; that's the nature of the unified theory.</p>
</div>

<div class="timeline-split timeline-split--note" role="note">
  <i class="ph ph-info timeline-split-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i>
  <div class="timeline-split-body">
    <strong class="timeline-split-label">Fan interpretation — not official canon</strong>
    <p class="timeline-split-note">The games in this list were made by different studios across different eras and were never intended to share a single continuity. This timeline is a fan theory that treats all entries as one interconnected story. Flashback chapters from <em>Chronicles</em> and <em>Anniversary</em> are placed at the points in Lara's life when they occur, rather than the release dates of the games they appear in.</p>
  </div>

<div class="timeline">

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--flashback">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr4/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">1984 — Teenage years</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr4/">Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--flashback">Flashback</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc"><strong>Cambodia prologue.</strong> A sixteen-year-old Lara joins renowned archaeologist Werner Von Croy on an expedition to Angkor Wat. Inside a booby-trapped temple, Lara ignores Von Croy's warnings and dons the Amulet of Horus, unleashing the wrath of a god. Von Croy loses his leg saving her and vows she will pay for her recklessness. This formative moment — and the guilt it leaves on both sides — defines their relationship for the rest of their lives.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--flashback">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr5/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Chronicles" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Young adulthood</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr5/">Tomb Raider: Chronicles</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--flashback">Flashback — Ireland</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc"><strong>The Supernatural.</strong> A young Lara travels to Ireland at the request of Father Dunstan, who is beset by supernatural forces he cannot explain. She investigates ancient ruins haunted by demons and undead — her first brush with the truly inexplicable. The experience cements her belief that the world holds real, dangerous power beneath its myths.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr2013/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider (2013)" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Origin</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr2013/">Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Twenty-one-year-old Lara joins the <em>Endurance</em> expedition, hunting for the lost kingdom of Yamatai in the Devil's Sea. A storm wrecks the ship and strands the crew on a mysterious island ruled by the cult of the Solarii Brotherhood. Hunted, captured, and pushed to the absolute limit, Lara discovers what she is capable of — and can never return to who she was before. This is where the Tomb Raider is born.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/rottr/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Rise of the Tomb Raider" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">After Yamatai</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/rottr/">Rise of the Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Still haunted by what she witnessed on Yamatai, Lara pursues her late father's obsession: the Divine Source, a mythical artefact said to grant immortality. Her search takes her to Syria and then into the frozen Siberian wilderness, where she must reach the lost city of Kitezh before Trinity — a ruthless paramilitary order — can claim its power. Lara begins to understand that her father's work was not madness.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/sottr/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Shadow of the Tomb Raider" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">After Kitezh</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/sottr/">Shadow of the Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara steals a Mayan dagger from a Trinity site in Mexico, inadvertently triggering a series of apocalyptic natural disasters. To undo what she has started, she must find Paititi, a hidden city deep in the Peruvian jungle that holds the key to stopping the cataclysm. For the first time, Lara must truly reckon with the destruction her obsession has caused. Developed by Eidos Montréal.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--flashback">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tranniversary/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Anniversary" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">A memory</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tranniversary/">Tomb Raider: Anniversary</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--flashback">Flashback — Nepal</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc"><strong>The crash.</strong> Somewhere in her past — perhaps resurfacing now as she processes the losses of the Survivor years — Lara carries the memory of a mountain, a wrecked plane, and a temple. Her mother, Amelia, stepped onto an ancient dais and vanished through a portal of light. Lara was left alone in the cold. She has never stopped looking for an explanation. This memory will not find its answer until <em>Legend</em> and <em>Underworld</em>.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--flashback">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr5/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Chronicles" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Before the career</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr5/">Tomb Raider: Chronicles</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--flashback">Flashback — Rome</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc"><strong>The Philosopher's Stone.</strong> A teenage Lara, with only her wits and a lockpick, sets out to steal the Philosopher's Stone from the vaults of Italian mobster Gianni Bartoli — assisted by (and frequently at odds with) Bartoli's hired gun, Larson Conway. It is Lara's first real infiltration, her first exposure to the criminal world of artefact trafficking, and a blueprint for the professional recklessness that defines her career.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--merged">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tranniversary/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider / Anniversary / Legacy of Atlantis" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">First professional expedition</p>
    <p class="timeline-title">
      <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr1/">Tomb Raider</a> &amp; <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tranniversary/">Anniversary</a> &amp; <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr-legacy-of-atlantis/">Legacy of Atlantis</a>
      <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span>
      <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--merged">Merged</span>
    </p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Arms dealer Jacqueline Natla hires Lara to recover three pieces of the Scion of Atlantis, an artefact of near-limitless power. The hunt takes her from the mountains of Peru through ancient Greece and Egypt before culminating on the lost continent of Atlantis itself. Lara defeats Natla and destroys what she has found — but the consequences of Atlantis will reach forward through time. <em>Tomb Raider</em> (1996) and <em>Anniversary</em> (2007) tell versions of this same story; <em>Legacy of Atlantis</em> (TBA) continues it directly.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr2/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider II" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Shortly after Atlantis</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr2/">Tomb Raider II</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara pursues the legendary Dagger of Xian, an artefact said to turn its wielder into a dragon. The trail runs from the Great Wall of China through the canals of Venice, a sunken ocean liner in the South China Sea, and the Tibetan mountains — where she confronts crime lord Marco Bartoli and his Fiamma Nera cult. She is also, briefly, under siege in her own manor.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--flashback">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr5/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Chronicles" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">A memory</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr5/">Tomb Raider: Chronicles</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--flashback">Flashback — New York</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc"><strong>The skyscraper.</strong> Werner Von Croy has acquired an artefact Lara wants back. She infiltrates his heavily fortified New York skyscraper — scaling the outside of the building, evading laser grids and armed guards — to reclaim it. The mission encapsulates the cold war of respect and resentment between mentor and former student. It will not be their last confrontation.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr3/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider III" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Continuing</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr3/">Tomb Raider III</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Four artefacts carved from a meteorite that crashed in Antarctica millennia ago are scattered across the globe. Starting in India, Lara recovers them across the South Pacific, the Nevada desert, and London — in any order she chooses — before converging on an Antarctic research base where Dr. Willard's obsession with the meteorite's mutagenic properties has produced something monstrous.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--flashback">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr5/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Chronicles" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">A memory</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr5/">Tomb Raider: Chronicles</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--flashback">Flashback — Russia</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc"><strong>The Spear of Destiny.</strong> Lara boards a Cold War-era Soviet submarine to recover the Spear of Destiny — a relic that, according to legend, grants its possessor unstoppable military power. The mission plunges her into a claustrophobic maze of flooded corridors and Soviet-era security systems, pursued the whole way by armed soldiers who have no intention of letting her surface with what she came for.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr4/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Egypt</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr4/">Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">In present-day Egypt, Lara inadvertently breaks the seal on the tomb of Set, the Egyptian god of chaos — unleashing a force that begins to consume the country. Racing to contain the damage with Von Croy reluctantly at her side, she fights across pyramids, temples, and catacombs to re-imprison what she freed. The attempt leaves her apparently buried beneath a collapsing temple. The world believes Lara Croft is dead. <em>Note: the Chronicles framing story — Lara's friends sharing memories at Croft Manor — takes place in the aftermath of this event.</em></p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr6/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Resurfacing</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr6/">Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara surfaces in Paris, wanted for the murder of Werner Von Croy — framed by a shadowy organisation called the Cabal, who are hunting the Obscura Paintings and the secret of an ancient alchemist. She forms an uneasy alliance with mercenary Kurtis Trent as the investigation moves from Paris to Prague. Core Design's final entry in the franchise, released after a troubled development that cut much of its intended scope.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/trlegend/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Legend" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Present day</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/trlegend/">Tomb Raider: Legend</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">A trail of broken sword fragments — each linked to a different corner of the globe — leads Lara to a truth she has been circling her entire career: what really happened to her mother in those Himalayan mountains. The search takes her from Bolivia to Tokyo, Ghana, Kazakhstan, Nepal, and Cornwall, as the myth of Excalibur and the realm of Avalon collapse into devastating personal history. Crystal Dynamics' debut with the franchise.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/trunderworld/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Underworld" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Shortly after Legend</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/trunderworld/">Tomb Raider: Underworld</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Directly continuing from <em>Legend</em>, Lara dives beneath the Mediterranean in search of the Norse underworld of Niflheim — and the answer to what became of her mother. The investigation carries her to Thailand, Mexico, the Arctic, and the ruins of Croft Manor before delivering a resolution to the mystery that has haunted Lara since childhood. The Legend arc ends here.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/lc-guardian/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Lara Croft and the Guardian of Light" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy"
         onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='{{ site.baseurl }}/images/lc-guardian/cover.png'">
    <p class="timeline-year">After Underworld</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/lc-guardian-of-light/">Lara Croft and the Guardian of Light</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--spinoff">Spin-off</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara discovers the Mirror of Smoke, a Mayan artefact imprisoning the evil spirit Xolotl. When Xolotl escapes and possesses an unfortunate mercenary, Lara must team up with the ancient warrior Totec — its guardian — to recover the mirror and re-imprison what was freed. A departure from the mainline formula: a top-down co-operative action game, though its events slot into Lara's ongoing story.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/lc-temple/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Lara Croft and the Temple of Osiris" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy"
         onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='{{ site.baseurl }}/images/lc-temple/cover.png'">
    <p class="timeline-year">After Guardian of Light</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/lc-temple-of-osiris/">Lara Croft and the Temple of Osiris</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--spinoff">Spin-off</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">In Egypt, Lara and fellow archaeologist Carter Bell unwittingly free the Egyptian god Set — again — and find themselves reluctantly allied with two of Osiris's champions to reassemble the god's scattered remains before Set can claim dominion over the world. A larger-scale co-operative successor to <em>Guardian of Light</em>, expanding the formula to four players.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item timeline-item--upcoming">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr-catalyst/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Catalyst" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy"
         onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr-catalyst/cover.png'">
    <p class="timeline-year">TBA</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr-catalyst/">Tomb Raider: Catalyst</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--upcoming">Upcoming</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Details are not yet public. In the context of this unified reading, <em>Catalyst</em> is anticipated to bring Lara's long story toward a new chapter — a culmination of threads that have run through every era. Where it ultimately sits in this continuity remains to be seen.</p>
  </div>
