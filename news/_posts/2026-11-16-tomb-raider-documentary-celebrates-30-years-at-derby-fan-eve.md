Amazon Game Studios and Crystal Dynamics have released a new mini-documentary episode for Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, covering the franchise's 30th anniversary and the TR30 fan event held in Derby, England, in October, according to a release.

The official video is available on the Play Tomb Raider YouTube channel and runs for 28 minutes and 48 seconds. According to the release, the event brought together hundreds of fans, original Tomb Raider developers, Lara Croft voice actors and models, and members of the teams behind Legacy of Atlantis, in the city where the series began.

The documentary looks at what the franchise means to the people who made it, as well as the community fans have built around Lara Croft's journeys. "Without the fans, Tomb Raider wouldn't still be alive today," said Alison Carroll, who was a Lara Croft model from 2008 to 2010. "The fans are everything, and the fact that there are 400 people here coming to celebrate Tomb Raider speaks for itself."

Lara Croft voice actress Alix Wilton Regan said the character has lasted because she represents qualities people are drawn to. "I think Lara Croft has endured as a character for so long because she's lots of things that we find attractive in the world in general, right?" Wilton Regan said. "We all want to be Lara Croft."

The new video is the latest in a mini-documentary series on the making of Legacy of Atlantis. Earlier episodes have introduced Lara Croft and covered the game's versions of Peru, Greece and Egypt, as well as its puzzles, combat, traversal and characters including Natla and Larson.

According to the release, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is a single-player, narrative-driven action-adventure reimagining of the 1996 debut title. Players take on the role of Lara Croft on a globe-trotting quest to discover the lost secrets of Atlantis.

The publisher described the game as featuring 'jaw-dropping visuals', 'modernized gameplay' and 'new surprises that honor the spirit of the original game', along with redesigned versions of familiar puzzles and the series' well-known T-Rex encounter. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will launch on February 12th 2027 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam and Nintendo Switch 2.
