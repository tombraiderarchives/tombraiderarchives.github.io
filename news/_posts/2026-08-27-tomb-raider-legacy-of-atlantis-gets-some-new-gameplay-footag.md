Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis got showcased at length by Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog on the XBOX stage at Gamescom 2026.

For a good fifteen minutes, we got to watch Lara in the remake of her first adventure. The legendary video game heroine swims, solves puzzles, and battles dinosaurs.

The footage isn’t of the highest quality, but it offers a glimpse into the various aspects of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, which is set to be released on February 12, 2027.
