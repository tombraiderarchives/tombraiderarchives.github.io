The Epic Games Store has refreshed its ongoing mystery giveaway promotion with another double freebie, replacing last week’s Telltale Batman collection and Sunderfolk. This time, PC players can claim Tomb Raider I-III Remastered alongside the colorful puzzle adventure Down in Bermuda completely free for a limited time.

The biggest highlight is easily the Tomb Raider collection from Aspyr, which bundles together remastered versions of the first three classic Lara Croft adventures. The package includes Tomb Raider I, II, and III, along with all expansion content and secret levels released for the original games.

Beyond simply re-releasing the classics, the remastered collection introduces several modern improvements. Players can instantly switch between the original visuals and upgraded graphics at any time, while updated control schemes add smoother movement and full camera control. Additional features include a new challenge mode and various quality-of-life improvements designed to make the classic action-adventure titles more accessible to modern audiences.

The games still retain the core experience fans remember from the 1990s, featuring exploration-heavy gameplay, environmental puzzles, dangerous traps, and plenty of platforming across ancient ruins and forgotten civilizations. Players once again step into the role of Lara Croft as she uncovers hidden secrets, battles mythical creatures, and searches for powerful artifacts around the world.

The second free title, Down in Bermuda, offers a much more relaxed experience. Developed by Yak & Co., the game follows an adventurous pilot trapped inside the mysterious Bermuda Triangle after becoming stranded in a strange time bubble.

Players explore six colorful islands filled with puzzles, hidden items, and secrets. Progression revolves around solving environmental challenges, cracking codes, collecting magical orbs, and uncovering clues that gradually reveal the mystery behind the islands and the pilot’s predicament.

The game’s bright visuals and lighthearted tone make it a strong contrast to Tomb Raider’s more dangerous adventures, giving players two very different experiences in this week’s giveaway lineup.

Both Tomb Raider I-III Remastered and Down in Bermuda are now free to claim through the Epic Games Store until May 28. As always, once claimed, the games remain permanently attached to your Epic library. A brand-new mystery giveaway is expected to replace them once the promotion ends next week.
