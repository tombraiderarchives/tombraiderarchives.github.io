Save 86% on nine Tomb Raider games in this huge Steam bundle.

Tomb Raider fans can save nearly $100 on nine games - and all previously released DLC - in this can't-miss Steam deal that comes in just under $16. But this offer won't last forever, so don't wait too long.

Gamers were first introduced to Lara Croft's adventures in Tomb Raider back in 1996, and the series has continued to grow its fanbase over the years, despite a few ups and downs. Recently, fans were teased with an OG Easter egg in an upcoming and highly anticipated remake.

In 2013, the franchise rebooted with what became known as the “Survivor Trilogy”, following Lara's fight to survive on the mysterious island of Yamatai. Now, with Tomb Raider celebrating its 30th anniversary, you can grab the Survivor Trilogy and six remastered classics in a huge Steam sale for a limited time.

The bundle in question, the ‘Tomb Raider Adventure Bundle,’ costs just $15.70/£13.08, instead of $112.46/£93.71; that’s an 86% saving. The deal ends on November 6, 2026.

Yes, absolutely. While the original games have understandably aged over time, the remasters bring improved visuals and modernised control options, making them more accessible to today's players without losing the feel of the original adventures.

As for the Survivor Trilogy, while these games weren't everyone's cup of tea, they're still considered solid Tomb Raider entries. On the review aggregate website Metacritic, Tomb Raider (2013) scored 86, Rise of the Tomb Raider also scored 86, and Shadow of the Tomb Raider scored 77.

Whether you're a veteran fan of the series or a newcomer, this bundle is a great way to catch up or get acquainted with Lara Croft's adventures, both old-school and modern. The original six games offer a fantastic sense of wonder, brilliant puzzles, fun combat and classic tomb raiding, even if their gameplay feels clunkier than that of the newer entries.

The Survivor Trilogy also captures that sense of adventure, with puzzles, combat and tomb raiding, but its gameplay is more refined, with a greater emphasis on cinematic set pieces and storytelling. Together, these nine games should certainly keep you busy until the next two adventures in the series arrive.

The reimagining of the original game, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, arrives on February 12, 2027. Meanwhile, Tomb Raider: Catalyst is expected to arrive in 2028, although a specific release date hasn't been confirmed. The game is also described as continuing Lara's story from the Survivor Trilogy.
