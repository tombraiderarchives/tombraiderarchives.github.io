Intel has relaunched its Gamer Days Bundle promotion. This time, Intel is offering buyers two free games with qualifying processors, graphics products, and prebuilt systems.

Running from August 10th through September 13th, the bundle includes full game downloads for STAR WARS: Galactic Racer and Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis. Customers have until October 31st to redeem their game codes after making a purchase. Depending on where you buy, redemption requires a Master Key, a system hardware scan, or basic purchase verification with proof of purchase. Only one offer is available per qualifying product and returned or cancelled orders will lose eligibility.

The offer covers a wide range of hardware, including Arc G3 graphics, Core 200H laptop processors, 13th and 14th Gen Core chips, and Core Ultra 100, 200, and 300 H-series processors. Newer Core Ultra Plus models and MSI handhelds featuring Lunar Lake SOCs are also on the eligible list alongside various suffix variants like K, KF, H, and so on.
