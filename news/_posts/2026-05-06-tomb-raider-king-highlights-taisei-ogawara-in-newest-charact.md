A new character trailer has been released for the upcoming Japanese dub of the Tomb Raider King South Korean animated series, highlighting the formidable Taisei Ogawara. The series will premiere in Japan this coming July 2026.

A previous character trailer spotlighting the protagonist, Goriki Ryoga, was released last week.

Seung-Wook Woo is directing the Tomb Raider King animated series and is also credited as the series composer. Hyun-Joung Lee is serving as the character designer, while STUDIO EEK is in charge of the animation production.

The animation is based on the manhwa webtoon series drawn by 3B2S, which adapts the web novel written by SAN.G. REDICE STUDIO’s Yuns is credited with the webtoon’s adaptation. The webtoon ran for two seasons from June 30, 2019 to March 27, 2023, with 16 total volumes being released. Tapas releases the webtoon in English digitally.

In Japan, the webtoon series is serialized on Piccoma, with KADOKAWA publishing volumes under the MF Comics imprint.

The Tomb Raider King web novel was first published on KakaoPage in November 2016 and concluded in May 2018. It is available to read in English on Wuxiaworld and Tapas.

Yen Press releases the Tomb Raider King webtoon series in English and describes the main synopsis as:

In the year 2025, mysterious tombs appeared all over the world, each housing an ancient relic granting supernatural abilities. The lucky few who quickly obtained these relics ruled over powerful monopolies. Others came to serve them, such as Jooheon Suh, a “raider” who hid in tombs to steal relics. Betrayed by his employer, Jooheon is left to die but instead finds himself transported 15 years in the past.
