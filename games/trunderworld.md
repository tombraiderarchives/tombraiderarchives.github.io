At the start of the story, Lara discovers that her mothers final destination was not Avalon but the Norse underworld Helheim. To reach it, she must assemble the pieces of Mjolnir, the hammer of Thor. Her journey pulls her through myths of the Hindu underworld Bhogavati, the Mayan realm Xibalba, and the Norse lands of Niflheim, Valhalla, and Helheim. Each area challenges her with puzzles, enemies, and ancient traps.

The long conflict with Amanda Evert returns as a major focus in Underworld. Lara is supported by Zip, Alister, and Winston as the mystery grows, tying back to events from Legend and Anniversary.
