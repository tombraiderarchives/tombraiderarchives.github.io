An animated anthology series commissioned to mark the 10th anniversary of Tomb Raider. Eight short episodes, each produced by a different animation studio, resulting in wildly different visual styles — from cel-shaded manga-inspired animation to gritty realism to outright comedy.

Released free online via Gametrailers.com, ReVisioned was an early experiment in web-based serialised animation. Every studio interpreted Lara Croft through their own lens, making the series a fascinating collection of creative visions. It has since become a collector's item among dedicated fans.
