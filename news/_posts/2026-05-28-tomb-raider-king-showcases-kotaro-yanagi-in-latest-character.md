A new character trailer has been unveiled for the upcoming Japanese language dub of Tomb Raider King, showcasing Kotaro Yanagi. The Japanese dub of the South Korean animated series will begin airing in Japan in July 2026.

Seung-Wook Woo is directing the Tomb Raider King animated series and is also credited as the series composer. Hyun-Joung Lee is serving as the character designer, while STUDIO EEK is in charge of the animation production.

The original web novel was written by SAN.G and first published on KakaoPage in November 2016, ending in May 2018. It is available to read in English on Wuxiaworld and Tapas.

The series was then adapted into a manhwa webtoon series drawn by 3B2S, with REDICE STUDIO’s Yuns credited with the webtoon’s adaptation. It ran for two seasons from June 30, 2019 to March 27, 2023, with 16 total volumes being released. Tapas publishes the webtoon in English digitally.

In Japan, the webtoon series is serialized on Piccoma and Kadokawa publishes the volumes under the MF Comics imprint.

Yen Press releases the Tomb Raider King webtoon series in English print and describes the main synopsis as:

In the year 2025, mysterious tombs appeared all over the world, each housing an ancient relic granting supernatural abilities. The lucky few who quickly obtained these relics ruled over powerful monopolies. Others came to serve them, such as Jooheon Suh, a “raider” who hid in tombs to steal relics. Betrayed by his employer, Jooheon is left to die but instead finds himself transported 15 years in the past.
