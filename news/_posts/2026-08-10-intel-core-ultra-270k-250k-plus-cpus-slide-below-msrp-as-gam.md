Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus & Ultra 5 250K Plus are now available below MSRP and come with a brand new game bundle.

Last month, Intel CPUs received a price hike, which was unexpected. This led to higher prices on the recently launched Core Ultra 7 270K Plus and the Core Ultra 5 250K Plus. These two CPUs are designed with value in mind, and these price hikes meant that the value proposition was reduced compared to what reviewers showed.

Intel blamed rising supply chain costs as the primary reason for the increased prices, and there might be some truth to it given the fact that almost every single component is facing shortages & that has resulted in prices going out of control.

Now, a month later, it looks like gamers will be able to buy both chips either at or below MSRP. As part of Intel's "Gamer Days" promotion, several Intel-powered PCs, including CPUs, are now selling at much lower prices.

Starting with the Core Ultra 7 270K Plus, this chip is now available for $290 US, which is $10 US off its original MSRP and $49 US off its new MSRP. The chip originally launched at $299, but price hikes have pushed the price to $339. This means that the 270K Plus is once again a very competitive chip vs the AMD Ryzen stack, which continues to expand.

Meanwhile, the Core Ultra 5 250K Plus, which launched at an MSRP of $199 US, is now available for $194 US (for KF) and $209 US (for K). The 250K launched at $199 but was pushed to $219 US, while the KF launched at $199 and was pushed to $204 US.

In addition to the discounted deals, Intel has also launched its latest "Gamer Days" bundle that applies to Intel CPUs, Intel Laptops, Intel Handhelds, and Intel Arc platforms (Mobile/Desktops).

The new bundle follows a time period from August 10, 2026, to September 13, 2026. The qualified products need to be purchased within this period to be eligible for two games, which include the upcoming Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis and Star Wars: Galactic Racer. Both of these games have a combined value of $119.98 US. The redemption period will last till October 31, 2026, and you can visit https://softwareoffer.intel.com/ for more information on the redemption process.
