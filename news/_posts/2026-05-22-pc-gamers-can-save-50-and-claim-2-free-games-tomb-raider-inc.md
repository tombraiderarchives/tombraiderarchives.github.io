If you head over to the Epic Games Store, you can pick up two limited-time free games that are yours to keep on PC.

Well, you’re technically getting four free games as one release is a bundle of three all-time great action-adventure classics.

Right now, you can claim Tomb Raider I-III Remastered at no cost whatsoever by visiting the game’s Epic Games Store listing.

This one typically retails for $29.99 and, of course, only landed back in 2024.

It bundles together Lara Croft’s original three adventures, including the Unfinished Business, Golden Mask, and The Lost Artifact expansions.

Gamers should expect improved visuals, modernised controls, and a new free challenge mode.

That allows you to replay levels with customised modifiers, which offer 10 new outfits as a reward, with those outfits enhancing Lara’s abilities.

While the graphics have been improved, the collection includes an option that lets you toggle on those original, nostalgic polygonal graphics.

There’s arguably no better time to try out these originals with Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, a remake of the franchise’s original game, due to land later this year.

If you’ve already played Tomb Raider I-III Remastered, you can also pick up Down In Bermuda for free.

Down In Bermuda is heavily discounted pretty often but it does command an RRP of $19.99.

In this 2021 adventure from Yak & Co, players step into the shoes of an aviator who is stranded on an island after encountering a storm in the Bermuda Triangle.

You’ll have to “unravel the island's secrets” and “overcome creatures of the deep” in this cosy mystery.

For the most part, Down In Bermuda tasks you with solving puzzles as you work your way through six islands with each one bringing you one step closer to finding your way home.

This one boasts a ‘Very Positive’ review score on PC storefront Steam, with one player writing, “A light and easy hidden object game. Not super challenging, just kind of relaxing. Great for an in-between game.”

You should be able to complete it in under five hours, whereas Tomb Raider I-III Remastered will demand at least 40 hours of your time, likely far more.

Both games are free from now until 28 May at 4pm BST, so you’d best claim them sooner rather than later.

Once claimed, they’re yours to keep. As a reminder, the only catch is that you can only secure a PC copy.

On Thursday, 28 May when the games do expire, two new games will replace them as the next batch of free offerings - and we’ll bring news of that change to you when it arrives.
