Xbox's Black Friday sale is underway, and here are nine games you can pick up for less than $15.

It may feel like the entirety of the world is playing Grand Theft Auto VI, and that assertion isn’t far wrong.

If you’re not playing Rockstar Games’ behemoth, though, you’re not alone; GTA VI isn’t everyone’s cup of tea, and for some, it simply isn’t in their price range at £69.99 / $79.99 for the game’s most basic edition.

Thankfully, there’s plenty out there that you can pick up for a steal - especially on Xbox, which has officially launched its Black Friday sale.

We’ve scoured the digital storefront for our best picks; here are nine games you can purchase right now for less than $15.

Save 80 percent on the Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy, which includes three brilliant games for the measly price of $9.99. This is a reboot trilogy, so you don’t need any prior experience with the character; although, let’s be honest, who isn’t familiar with Lara Croft? Tomb Raider serves as an origin story before Lara grows into a fully-fledged hero in Rise of the Tomb Raider and Shadow of the Tomb Raider.

Rise is often considered the trilogy’s best entry, but I’d argue it’s Shadow. While we may have a new Lara in the upcoming 2028 launch Tomb Raider: Catalyst, the game will follow on from this particular trilogy, so there’s no better time to catch up.

Unlike previous entries, Metro Exodus breaks away from a linear formula to introduce some semi-open-world hubs. If you want to shoot your way through a high-octane post-apocalyptic landscape, this 20 to 25 hour experience should keep you satisfied.

Hogwarts Legacy also boasts a 90 percent discount in Xbox’s latest sale, allowing you to sample the Wizarding World RPG ahead of the premiere of HBO’s Harry Potter TV reboot, which actually takes inspiration from the world design of Avalanche Studios’ best-selling title.

The game sees you join Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry in your fifth year, with the playable character able to tap into a powerful ancient magic. With a sequel widely believed to be announced soon, it’s a good time to catch up - and while the open-world is a tad repetitive, exploring Hogwarts itself is a delight.

If you want something a little bit less mainstream, then Cult of the Lamb might be it. Massive Monster and Devolver Digital’s 2022 roguelike remains as brilliant today as it was when it first launched four years ago - and if you like what you play, there are tons of paid DLCs you can add on to extend the base game experience.

It essentially sees you run a cult of, you guessed it, lambs, with the player needing to keep their cult fed, watered, healthy, and warm. That means keeping everyone in line and contributing to your budding society. If they don’t and you’re low on food, they might end up as your next meal.

If GTA VI is a little out of your price range, why not go back and revisit what is arguably the best Grand Theft Auto game out there, prior to GTA VI’s release. GTA IV is a steal at 65 percent off, with the game following the misadventures of Niko Bellic.

Taking place across Liberty City, Bellic sets out in pursuit of the American Dream like so many others. But he soon becomes enraged in Liberty City’s underworld with the player engages in all kinds of wrongdoing. The density of offerings in Liberty City felt so revolutionary back in 2008, and it’s still a joy to play today.

Final Fantasy VII Revelation is on the way in 2027, so here’s your chance to catch up on the latest instalment, Final Fantasy VII Rebirth, which actually only landed on Xbox earlier this year, having launched on PlayStation back in 2024. With 85 percent off then, that’s a pretty major launch-year discount.

If you love sprawling RPGs, Final Fantasy VII Rebirth is for you. In this particular entry, Cloud Strife and company depart Midgar in pursuit of Sephiroth. With a runtime of 50 to 160 hours, you’re getting plenty of bang for your buck.
