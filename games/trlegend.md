Crystal Dynamics' debut Tomb Raider rebooted the franchise with a slicker, more cinematic Lara. Legend reimagined her backstory — her mother disappeared in a plane crash in Nepal when Lara was a child, and the mystery of that disappearance drives the plot as Lara investigates a series of relics connected to Arthurian legend.

The game was widely praised for revitalising the series after Angel of Darkness, introducing fluid movement mechanics and a more likeable, character-driven Lara. It set the foundation for the Legend Era trilogy.
