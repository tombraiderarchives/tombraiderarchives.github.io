This is clearly information that shouldn’t have been leaked this way. An article on Amazon’s official news site published in December 2025 was updated this week. It’s the one about the upcoming games Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis and Tomb Raider: Catalyst.

As we all know, Legacy of Atlantis is coming to PC, Switch 2, PS5, and XBOX Series on February 12, 2027. However, we have very little information about the second game, Tomb Raider: Catalyst. The article states that a Nintendo Switch 2 edition is indeed in the works, even though it had never been announced.

Unless this is a mistake on the company’s part, we can conclude that players will be able to play Tomb Raider: Catalyst on Nintendo’s latest console in 2028. This information will need to be officially confirmed by either the manufacturer or the publisher.
