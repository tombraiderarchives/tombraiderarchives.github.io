A Netflix animated series developed by Powerhouse Animation — the studio behind Castlevania and Blood of Zeus — and written by Tasha Huo. Set after the events of the Survivor Era game trilogy, the series follows a more experienced but emotionally scarred Lara as she chases a powerful artefact stolen from the Croft family vault.

The hunt takes Lara across multiple continents, forcing her to confront questions about identity and purpose — what it truly means to be a Tomb Raider. Hayley Atwell voices Lara, bringing warmth and intensity to the role. Powerhouse's signature fluid action animation translates the franchise's energy exceptionally to screen.
