The original Tomb Raider was released on PlayStation 30 years ago in 1996. Today, you can relive pretty much all of Lara Croft’s classic adventures through their ports and remasters on Switch and Switch 2. Today a new trailer has been shared showcasing the series’ biggest titles from over the years and their new editions. Watch it above.

Today marks the 30th Anniversary of the Tomb Raider series! In 1996, 30 years ago today, the original Tomb Raider game was released. Who knew it would become the massive franchise it is today or that Lara Croft would become a household name synonymous with adventure?

The original games, like the treasures Lara uncovers, have not been lost to time, they were only waiting for someone to rediscover them. Dive back into the adrenaline-pumping action, explore the mysteries once more behind ancient civilizations and nefarious secret societies, and re-experience the moment when Lara becomes the Tomb Raider she’s destined to be!
