A new physical Tomb Raider release has officially been confirmed, with Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection getting Standard and Collector's Editions for the Nintendo Switch 2. Distribution is being handled by Limited Run, giving this Tomb Raider release another way for fans to revisit one of Lara Croft's most acclaimed modern adventures with plenty of goodies.

Originally released in 2015, Rise of the Tomb Raider served as the middle chapter of Crystal Dynamics' Survivor trilogy and built heavily on the foundation established by the 2013 reboot. Critics and fans generally praised its larger environments, exploration, tombs, and impressive visuals, with the expanded 20 Year Celebration edition later earning an 88 Metascore on PS4. Interestingly, the "20 Year" name originally celebrated the Tomb Raider franchise turning 20 in 2016. Another ten years later, Lara is now celebrating her 30th anniversary, making this a decade-old anniversary edition that gets a second life during a completely different milestone.
