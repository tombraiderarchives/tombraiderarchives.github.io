Part of the Tomb Raider I–III Remastered collection released 14 February 2024 by Aspyr Media. The 1996 original is rebuilt with high-resolution textures, modern lighting, and updated character models — while the level geometry and gameplay remain 100% faithful. Players can toggle between remastered and original 1996 visuals at any time with a single button, and choose between modern or classic tank controls.
