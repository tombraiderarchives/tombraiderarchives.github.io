Summer is in full swing, and for our favourite hardworking mother-of-three, Helen Skelton, that means a killer seasonal wardrobe.

The former Blue Peter presenter, 42, was the image of Lara Croft on Thursday when she shared a slew of snaps from her recent filming escapade.

For the occasion, Helen slipped on a fabulous pair of khaki shorts, an oversized knitted vest, accessorising her look with a pair of brown sunglasses and a tatten sun visor.

Captioning the post, Helen penned: "Walked. Talked and filmed. Great views. Good company, topped off by bargain flapjack and an honesty box coffee stop.

"Back out filming for @lostandfoundlakes... Trying to reunite people with their treasured possessions. If you have lost anything in the Lake District, let us know - don’t dm me. Link in bio. We will try and help. Equally, if you have found anything, let us know if we can get it back to its owner xx.

"Wore @varley missed @mrdanwalker for glorious nonsense chat but great reserve @daz156 Sophie, Kate and Shirley #lakes #hikes #fells #uk #summervibes."

Helen's vibrant blonde locks took front and centre of the updates, her polka-straight tresses cascading out of her visor.

"Beautiful lady," one fan penned in the comments. A second added: "Looking as wonderful as ever, smile reflects the great time you obviously had. Scenery just tops it all…" Meanwhile, a third penned: "Magnificent like you."

As it's the six-week holidays, it will mean that Helen has a slew of activities for her adorable three children, Ernie, Louis and Elsie, who she shares with her ex-husband Richie Myler, who are all under the age of 12.

Helen and her brood, as well as her parents, have enjoyed a cabin holiday at Forest Holidays. The On the Farm starlet shared special moments from the trip, including her fabulous fashion exploits in another post on Instagram.

One stand-out moment saw Helen rocking a black bikini while relaxing in a hot tub. Helen looked simply fabulous, the photo showing off her toned back and shoulders.
