This week's featured adoptable pet from the Animal Refuge League of Greater Portland is Lara Croft, a 2-month-old puppy who is ready to play.

Lara comes to Maine from a transport partner in Massachusetts and is part of a litter of five puppies that will become available for adoption starting Thursday, Nov. 19.

Matt Blanchard of the Animal Refuge League wanted to remind potential adopters that puppies require a lot of work and training, but he noted that Lara Croft is a sweetheart and that she already knows how to sit.

More information about Lara Croft is available on the Animal Refuge League's website, as long as she is still looking for her forever home.

Anyone who is interested in meeting Lara Croft or other adoptable pets can visit the Animal Refuge League's shelter at 217 Landing Road in Westbrook between 11 a.m. and 4 p.m., except on Wednesdays, when the shelter is closed for adoptions.

The Animal Refuge League of Greater Portland does adoptions on a first-come, first-served basis. On the days the shelter is open, sign-in sheets will be placed on the front door at 9 a.m. to track the order of arrival of adopters who show up early. People who arrive early are asked to remain on the campus once they sign in.

Dog owners who are interested in adopting dogs that are older than 6 months are required to have their dogs meet the adoptable pet before a new pet can be brought home.
