There’s also a new musical collaboration featuring lo-fi Tomb Raider tunes!

Tomb Raider turns 30 this year, and Crystal Dynamics is celebrating in style. Over the weekend in Derby, England, the TR30 Tomb Raider Day event was held for fans to celebrate the beloved series. The fun was all focused on Lara turning 30 and upcoming projects that will feature the character. First, here’s a new teaser for Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, which will be launching next year. You can watch it right here:

Along with the teaser, Crystal Dynamics has some special Tomb Raider-music projects to groove to. First off is the Tomb Raider Lo-Fi Collection, which is a special collaboration with That Norsk Chick. Songs from across the series are sampled and remixed, including works from the likes of Nathan McCree, Peter Connelly, Martin Iveson, Troels B. Folmann, Colin O’Malley, Jason Graves, Bobby Tahouri, Brian D’Oliveira, Wilbert Roget, II, and Paul Houseman. The tunes stretch from the original trilogy all the way through to the latest games. You can listen to it right here:

Beyond the lo-fi beats, Crystal Dynamics is also planning to release 500 tracks of music from across the series’ history via various streaming services. The company has partnered with Harry Hinds to get multiple soundtracks available in the coming weeks. Lots of exciting things for Tomb Raider fans to enjoy at the moment; if you’ve yet to get our impression of Legacy of Atlantis, be sure to check out this link.
