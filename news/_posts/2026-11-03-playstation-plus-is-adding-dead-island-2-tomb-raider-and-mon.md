November’s PlayStation Plus lineup arrives today, bringing three very different games to subscribers. From zombie-filled streets to archaeological adventures and strategic card battles, Sony’s latest selection offers something for several kinds of players.

Leading the lineup is Dead Island 2, the action RPG that transforms Los Angeles into a zombie-infested playground. Players explore recognizable locations across the city, complete missions, meet survivors and fight through hordes of enemies using an extensive selection of weapons.

The game can be played solo or through online cooperative multiplayer, making it an appealing option for subscribers looking for something to experience with friends.

Also joining the service is Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered, a collection of three classic Lara Croft adventures: The Last Revelation, Chronicles and The Angel of Darkness.

The remastered editions give players the option to switch between the games’ original graphics and updated visuals. It is an opportunity for longtime fans to revisit the series and for younger players to experience an earlier chapter in one of gaming’s best-known franchises.

Completing the lineup is Monster Train 2, a strategy game built around deck-building, tactical decisions and battles across multiple levels of a moving train.

All three games are available to eligible PlayStation Plus subscribers from November 3 through November 30, according to Sony’s official announcement.

For Moroccan PlayStation users, availability should be checked against their account’s regional store.
