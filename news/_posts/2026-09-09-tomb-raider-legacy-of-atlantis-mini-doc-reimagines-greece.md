Amazon Game Studios and Crystal Dynamics have released the eighth Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis mini-documentary, this time focusing on the Greece level shown behind closed doors at Gamescom.

The episode highlights the reimagined Sword of Damocles puzzle, expanded mythology, and how the team is updating one of Lara Croft’s most memorable early locations.

The new episode looks at how Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis builds out Greece with more architecture, mythology, and environmental variety than the original 1996 game.

Game Director Raul Sequeira notes that Greece lets the team lean into puzzles based on Greek mythology, while also giving the area a different visual identity from places like Peru.

The Sword of Damocles puzzle is one of the episode’s main focuses.

Crystal Dynamics is keeping the original’s sense of quiet exploration while adding more active sequences, including timed areas that push players to move with urgency.

Design Director Jason Epps said Greece is one of his favorite levels in the game, pointing to the scale and mystery of its hidden Grecian city.

The mini-doc also touches on how the remake balances the slower, lonely feel of classic Tomb Raider with more dynamic moments.

That means players can expect puzzle-solving, ancient spaces, and tense moments as Lara works through Greece’s ruins.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis launches February 12, 2027, for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam.

The Greece-focused mini-documentary is available now, alongside a new blog post with more details on the reimagined level.
