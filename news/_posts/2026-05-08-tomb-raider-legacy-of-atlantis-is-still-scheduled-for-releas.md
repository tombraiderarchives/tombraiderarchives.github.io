In recent weeks, numerous rumors have surfaced suggesting that Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis has been delayed until February 2027. So far, Crystal Dynamics has not confirmed this information… in fact, quite the opposite.

In a post on LinkedIn, Electric Square, a company specializing in game co-development, announced that it is currently working on the remake. The post makes it very clear that the game is still scheduled for 2026.

Of course, Electric Square isn’t necessarily aware of a potential delay and wouldn’t have the authority to announce such a change in any case. Only Crystal Dynamics will be able to comment on this in the near future.
