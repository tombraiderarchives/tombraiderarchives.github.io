Right now we are unquestionably in the midst of one of the most exciting eras in Tomb Raider history, with several incredible new projects to look forward to for the iconic franchise. Fans are counting down the days for Crystal Dynamics' upcoming Legacy of Atlantis remake, with Tomb Raider: Catalyst on deck to give gamers even more to look forward to over the next couple years.

Further, Prime Video's upcoming Tomb Raider series starring Sophie Turner, created by Fleabag's Phoebe Waller-Bridge, is set to bring Lara Croft back to live-action television for the first time since the 2018 film. Unfortunately, there's a bit of a wait in store for most of these highly anticipated projects, and fans will luckily be getting a surprise new next-gen remaster of one of the franchise's most beloved entries to keep them busy in the meantime (via Go Nintendo).

Arguably one of the most unique and beloved titles in franchise history, Tomb Raider (2013) is getting an official new next-gen remaster by way of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition physical release for the Nintendo Switch 2, available for pre-order tomorrow, Thursday, July 2, 2026.
