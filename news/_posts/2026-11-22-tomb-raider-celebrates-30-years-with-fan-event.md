A new mini-documentary episode spotlights fans of the franchise ahead of the Legacy of Atlantis launch on February 12, 2027.

Are you planning to play the new Tomb Raider game when it launches?

Crystal Dynamics released a Tomb Raider 30th Anniversary mini-documentary episode focused on fans, now airing on YouTube. The episode features hundreds of fans, original developers, Lara Croft voice actors and models, and members of the Legacy of Atlantis teams at a celebration in the city where the franchise began.

The series marks three decades of the franchise and offers players a look at the community behind it while previewing the upcoming game. The episode is part of a piece-by-piece rollout that reveals elements of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis ahead of its launch.

The celebration drew approximately 400 people, and the franchise's 30th anniversary is being marked alongside the game's scheduled launch on February 12, 2027, for PC and all three major consoles. Specific viewership numbers for the mini-documentary have not been disclosed.

Crystal Dynamics is a video game developer best known for its work on the modern Tomb Raider reboot series and the publisher of the 30th Anniversary documentary.

Alix Wilton Regan is a voice actress who has portrayed Lara Croft in Tomb Raider games and is featured in the anniversary episode.

Alison Carroll is a model who served as the Lara Croft model from 2008 to 2010 and appears in the anniversary episode.

The latest episode of the Tomb Raider 30th Anniversary mini-documentary, released on YouTube, centers on fans of the franchise. It brings together hundreds of fans, original Tomb Raider developers, Lara Croft voice actors and models including Alix Wilton Regan and Alison Carroll, and members of the Legacy of Atlantis teams.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is scheduled to launch on February 12, 2027.
