Lara Croft receives a commission from Jacqueline Natla, CEO of Natla Technologies, to recover a mysterious artefact hidden in a Peruvian tomb. What begins as a straightforward job quickly spirals into a globe-spanning conspiracy involving the Scion — an ancient relic tied to the lost continent of Atlantis.

Spanning 15 levels across Peru, Greece, Egypt, and Atlantis, the game introduced the world to Lara Croft and established the template for third-person action adventure games. Despite dated controls by modern standards, its blend of exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat was groundbreaking.
