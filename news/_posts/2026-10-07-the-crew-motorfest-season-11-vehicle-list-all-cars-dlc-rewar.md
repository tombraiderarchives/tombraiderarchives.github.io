Key Update: Season 11 introduces a brand-new island in Kauai, a Tomb Raider DLC collaboration, and over 20 new vehicles—including cars, bikes, planes, and helicopters.

Quick Answer: The Crew Motorfest Season 11 adds over 20 vehicles, a new island (Kauai), and a Tomb Raider DLC pack featuring exclusive themed rides.

The Crew Motorfest Season 11 is here, introducing a brand-new island in Kauai, a Tomb Raider DLC, and a plethora of new vehicles, including a plane.

Reliable leaks had already revealed most of the Season 11 cars, but we know have the full list of vehicles coming to the island.

Without further ado, let's find out all the vehicles added in The Crew Motorfest Season 11 update.

Over 20 vehicles will be added to The Crew Motorfest throughout Season 11, many of which are only available for those who purchase the Year 4 Pass or the Tomb Raider DLC Pack.

The Year 4 Pass costs $29.99 / €29.99 and includes all 21 vehicles coming to the game in Season 11.

The Tomb Raider Secrets of Ni’ihau DLC is bundled with the pass, and it features five exclusive vehicles you can use to explore the astonishing island of Ni'ihau.

Not all of the 21 vehicles coming to The Crew Motorfest in Season 11 have been revealed. Five vehicles from three different classes will be announced during Season 11.

Here are all the The Crew Motorfest Season 11 vehicles revealed so far:

Cadillac CT5-V Blackwing 2025 – Year 4 Pass Exclusive - November 11

Hennessey Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 The Exorcist 2022 – Summit Reward - October 21

Italdesign Zerouno 2017 - Year 4 Pass or Elite Bundle - December 23

Honda Africa Twin Adventure Sports ES Bike 2021 - Year 4 Pass or Tomb Raider DLC Pack - October 7

Mercedes-AMG G 63 6X6 2013 – Year 4 Pass or Tomb Raider DLC Pack - October 7

Suzuki Quadracer R450Z 2008 - Year 4 Pass or Tomb Raider DLC Pack - October 7

Kimberlite HP-18-160 Camina Plane 1968 - Year 4 Pass or Tomb Raider DLC Pack - October 7

Proto A500 Special Edition Helicopter 2016 – Year 4 Pass or Tomb Raider DLC Pack - October 7

These are all the confirmed cars arriving in The Crew Motorfest Season 11. We will update this article once the remaining vehicles are announced.
