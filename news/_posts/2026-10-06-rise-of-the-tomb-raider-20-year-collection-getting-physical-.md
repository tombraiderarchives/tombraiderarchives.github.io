Limited Run Games, a premium publisher of physical video games, in partnership with developer Aspyr® announced today that Rise of the Tomb Raider™: 20 Year Collection will be leaping its way to Nintendo® Switch™ 2, with physical Standard and Collector’s Editions available for pre-order starting October 9.

Heading back to one of Lara Croft’s earliest tomb raiding expeditions, this thrilling chapter of the 30-year-old franchise sees gamers battling the extremes of Siberia on her quest to the lost city of Kitezh, unlocking the mysteries of the past while trying to survive the relentless forces of the insidious Trinity.

As Lara races to uncover the Divine Source, said to harness the power of immortality, gamers will be treated to an unforgettable globe-trotting adventure, complete with jaw-dropping visuals, and adrenaline-fueled action.

With a choice between Standard and Collector’s Editions, both including physical Game Card, Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection features the full game, alongside the Season Pass featuring available content.

Explore Croft Manor in the new “Blood Ties” story, then defend it against a zombie invasion in “Lara’s Nightmare”. Survive extreme conditions and team up with a friend in the online Co-Op Endurance mode, and brave the new “Extreme Survivor” difficulty, a challenge for even the bravest of adventurers! In addition, fans are treated to an outfit and weapon inspired by Tomb Raider™ III, and 5 classic Lara skins. Existing DLC will challenge gamers to explore a new tomb that houses an ancient terror in Baba Yaga: The Temple of the Witch, and combat waves of infected predators in Cold Darkness Awakened.

Key features of Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection:

Lara’s Journey – Lara uncovers an ancient mystery that places her in the cross-hairs of a ruthless organization known as Trinity. As she races to find the secret before Trinity, the trail leads to a myth about the Lost City of Kitezh. Lara knows she must reach the Lost City and its hidden secrets before Trinity. With that, she sets out for Siberia on her first Tomb Raiding expedition.

Woman vs. Wild – In “Rise of the Tomb Raider,” Lara battles with not only enemies from around the world, but the world itself. Hunt animals to craft weapons and scavenge for rare resources in densely populated ecosystems. You’ll encounter beautifully hostile environments, full of treacherous conditions and unstable landscapes that will require Lara to push her limits to the very edge.

Guerilla Combat - Use the environment to your advantage, scale trees and dive underwater to avoid or takedown enemies, configure Lara’s gear, weapons, and ammo to suit your play style from stealth to guns blazing, craft explosives on the fly to sow chaos, and wield Lara’s signature combat bows and climbing axe.

Return to Tomb Raiding – Tombs are back, and they’re bigger and better than ever. In “Rise of the Tomb Raider” huge, awe-inspiring ancient spaces littered with deadly traps, solve dramatic environmental puzzles, and decipher ancient texts to reveal crypts as you take on a world filled with secrets to discover.

Details of the Standard and Collector’s Editions of Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection are as follows:

Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection - Standard Edition - Nintendo Switch 2 - MSRP: $49.99 / £45.00 / €55.95 / $70.99 CAD

Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection - Collector’s Edition - Nintendo Switch 2 - MSRP: $99.99 / £90.00 / €110.95 / $140.99 CAD

Pre-orders for Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Collection are open from October 9 and extend until November 15, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET.
