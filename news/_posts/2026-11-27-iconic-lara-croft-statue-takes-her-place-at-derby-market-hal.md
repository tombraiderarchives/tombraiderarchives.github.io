A nine-foot-tall statue of video game icon Lara Croft has been unveiled at Derby Market Hall, celebrating the city’s role in creating one of the world’s most recognisable gaming characters.

The fibre-glass statue has found a permanent home in Derby following a successful crowdfunding campaign led by Derby-born Tomb Raider fan Luke Earle, which attracted support from more than 200 fans from across the world and raised more than £33,000 towards its purchase.

Derby City Council supported the project with £10,000 of grant funding secured through the UK Shared Prosperity Fund, helping to fund the statue’s installation and the creation of a display that celebrates Derby’s connection to Lara Croft and the city’s contribution to the video games industry.

Lara Croft first appeared in the groundbreaking Tomb Raider game, developed by Core Design in Derby and released in 1996. The character went on to become a global cultural icon, starring in a series of bestselling games, films and other media enjoyed by millions of people worldwide.

The statue was previously displayed at Camden Market in London outside the Tomb Raider: The LIVE Experience attraction before being acquired for Derby through the crowdfunding campaign.

Councillor Gurkiran Kaur, Derby City Council Cabinet Member for City Centre, Culture and Tourism, said:

Lara Croft is one of Derby’s most famous cultural exports, so it's fitting that this statue has found a permanent home in the city where her story began.

Luke and the many supporters of this project have done an amazing job in bringing Lara to Derby. Working together, we’ve been able to create something that celebrates Derby’s creative legacy while giving visitors another reason to explore Derby Market Hall and the city centre.

We hope residents and visitors alike will enjoy seeing the statue and learning more about Derby’s role in the history of the video games industry.

Interpretive displays alongside the statue tell the story of Derby’s role in the development of the video games industry and the creation of one of gaming’s most famous characters. They also feature a trail that fans can follow, visiting locations around the city that have links to Lara.

The statue is expected to become a popular attraction for both residents and visitors, particularly Tomb Raider fans who already visit Derby to see sites connected with Lara Croft and the game’s origins.

The Lara Croft statue started its life in Camden Market, so it seems quite fitting that she’s been given a brand new home where anybody can come and see her in Derby Market Hall.

Over 200 people pledged to make this happen and most of them will be getting their names on the statue early next year. I've had loads of messages saying how excited people are to see it in person, so it’s wonderful to know she’ll be here waiting for them.

Heather Stevens, one of the level designers behind the original Tomb Raider and Tomb Raider II, visited Derby Market Hall to unveil Lara as the statue was installed in its new home.

Derby Market Hall is the perfect place for Lara. Her adventures have always been set among historic places and ancient treasures, so it feels like a fitting home for her.

Lara has become an icon recognised around the world, so it’s wonderful to see her here in Derby. I hope lots of people will visit the Market Hall to see her and celebrate this special part of the city’s history.

Fans of Lara can also enjoy the Lara Croft: 30 Years On – From Derby to Global Icon Opens in new tab exhibition at Derby Museums’ Pickford’s House until 31 July 2027. The exhibition, co-curated by Luke, features original Tomb Raider development materials on loan from former Core Design developers, memorabilia and collectables from private collections, and interactive activities.
