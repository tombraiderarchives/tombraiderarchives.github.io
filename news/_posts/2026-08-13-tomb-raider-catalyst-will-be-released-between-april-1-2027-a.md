There is still some confusion surrounding the release window for Tomb Raider: Catalyst. Though originally announced for 2027, this new installment was later pushed back to 2028. The information came from the CEO of Amazon Games, before being partially denied in a press release.

In its most recent financial report, Embracer Group lists this title for the 2027/2028 fiscal year. This means that Tomb Raider: Catalyst will arrive on our consoles between April 1, 2027, and March 31, 2028.

Developed using Unreal Engine 5 and set in northern India, this new Lara Croft adventure will definitely be released after the remake of the first game, which is expected on February 12, 2027.
