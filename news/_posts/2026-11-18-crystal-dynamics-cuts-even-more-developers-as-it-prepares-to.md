Crystal Dynamics is letting more people go. The studio says it’s only eight staff, but it includes designers and engineers. Making the cuts all the more confusing is that the studio is gearing up to release two major games, a remake of the original Tomb Raider and the first new sequel since 2018.

“After several rounds of layoffs at Crystal Dynamics, today it was my turn,” design manager Joshua Mitchell post on LinkedIn (via Vertical Slice). “My first layoff in 14 years making games. I got to work on Perfect Dark, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, and Tomb Raider: Catalyst. I loved every minute of it, and I’m grateful to everyone I got to build alongside. I’m going to miss this team.”

Legacy of Atlantis was revealed at The Game Awards 2025 and was originally due out sometime this year. It has since been delayed to 2027. Catalyst, meanwhile, has been pushed back until 2028, a decade since Shadow of the Tomb Raider. Crystal Dynamics shipped Marvel’s Avengers in 2020 and worked on Xbox’s now-canceled Perfect Dark reboot in-between.

It’s the fifth layoff since March 2025. Around 20 people were laid off in March of 2026, and dozens more in 2025. The cancelation of Perfect Dark certainly played a role, but Amazon, Tomb Raider‘s publisher, has also been backing away from AAA game development. Embracer purchased Crystal Dynamics in 2022. Current CEO Phil Rogers said over the summer that Amazon’s own recent cuts and restructuring around its gaming division have impacted the Tomb Raider franchise.
