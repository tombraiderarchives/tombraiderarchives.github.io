Originally intended to be the final Tomb Raider game, The Last Revelation is set almost entirely in Egypt and features an ambitious interconnected level structure where areas revisited later in the game have changed. A young Lara Croft accidentally releases the Egyptian god Set, and must race to re-imprison him before he destroys the world.

The game ends with Lara's apparent death, leading many to believe the series was finished. The Egypt setting gave the developers room to create some of the series' most visually striking levels.
