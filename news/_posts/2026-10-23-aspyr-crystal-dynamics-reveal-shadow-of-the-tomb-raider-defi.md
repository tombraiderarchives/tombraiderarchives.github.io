Experience Lara Croft’s thrilling origin story across three epic games with the Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy collection!

AUSTIN, Texas – Oct. 22, 2026 – Surprise, TOMB RAIDER fans! Aspyr and Crystal Dynamics are excited to announce that Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, the epic conclusion to the Survivor Trilogy, is available now on Nintendo Switch 2 for $39.99, with a 10% launch discount through November 4. Discover the incredible final chapter of the gripping saga that established Lara Croft as the Tomb Raider she was destined to become, complete with all DLCs, challenge tombs, side missions, two-player online co-op and more!

Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition Launch Trailer: YouTube Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy Trailer: YouTube

Nintendo Switch 2 players can experience the entire Survivor Trilogy with the new Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy collection, which includes three award-winning games: Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration and Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, as well as their online modes and all DLC for each title. Play through Lara's complete origin story, featuring dozens of hours of breathtaking exploration, challenging puzzle-solving, and intense survival combat with one of gaming’s most iconic heroines. The Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy bundle is available now on Nintendo Switch 2 for $69.99, with a bonus 10% discount available for a limited time.

Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition is the award-winning action-adventure experience and the harrowing final chapter of the Survivor Trilogy. As you race to save the world from a Mayan apocalypse, you'll explore exotic locations, including the legendary Incan city of Paititi, while braving the unforgiving jungle and deadly tombs. Use advanced stealth techniques and an arsenal of weapons to stop the shadowy organization Trinity from stealing the power of an ancient god before it’s too late.

In addition to the base game, Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition includes all seven DLC challenge tombs, two-player online co-op, plus all downloadable side missions, weapons, outfits, and skills.

Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, priced at $39.99, and the Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy collection, priced at $69.99, are out now on Nintendo Switch 2, with a bonus 10% launch discount available through November 4.
