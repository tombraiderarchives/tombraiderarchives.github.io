The Crew Motorfest celebrated racing into Year 4 with a showcase highlighting exciting new content coming to Hawaii when Season 11 kicks off on October 7. Get ready to get your tires dirty in a season that is all about offroading as you explore a new free island Kauai, which is teeming with untamed wilderness waiting for you to blaze a trail. The new winch feature, available for a selection of vehicles,is ready to help you clear debris. The showcase also teased other features coming in the new season, including a Bugatti Tourbillon special event, wildlife on the archipelago, a new Trackforge kit, and more. Check it out:

The Crew Motorfest's map is expanding with Kauai, a free addition that's a raw, untamed paradise, unmarked and unclaimed, yours to conquer. The island is full of breathtaking natural beauty, taking you through swamps, canyons, hidden tunnels, and paths that have never been driven. With your new winch equipped, tear through fallen trees and debris, and drive through a completely untamed wilderness. Play the free Kauai Expeditions Playlist to carve the first roads across the island's coasts and cliffs. It's up to you to make Kauai the next great place of car culture celebration and prove the roads you make can handle the speed the Motorfest demands. The playlist features:

Eight main events Swamp Escape Jungle Extraction Green Dive Storm Valley Red Charge Sky Scouting Blue Pulse Kauai Grande Adventure

The Suzuki Jimmy 2018 Rally Raid is the reward for finishing the playlist.

Join Lara Croft on a hunt for her missing friend, who vanished while chasing a relic powerful enough to rewrite history. Time is running out, and Ni'ihau won't relinquish its secrets easily – the island has swallowed ships, expeditions, and legends, and it will fight back as every mile brings you closer to the truth. Lara will guide you from afar as you plunge into a stormy jungle littered with ancient ruins and deadly predators. Follow the clues and solve puzzles as you engage with an ancient enigma, and follow Ni'ihau's wildlife to uncover hidden paths – or outrun them when they turn on you.

Eight main events The Forbidden Island (available in demo) The Lost Land (available in demo) Sacred Fire Swamped Puzzle Surrounded The Temple of La’Amaomao The Goddess’ Wrath Time to Run

Bigger obstacles require bigger solutions, so starting in Season 11, The Crew Motorfest is adding a new winch feature to select vehicles. The winch turns every dead end into an opportunity to forge a new path as you haul fallen trees and other debris, complete puzzles, and help stuck vehicles go on their way again. To take on these towing challenges, there's a new vehicle that's more than up to the task: the Lamborghini Huracan Sterrato. Built to leave the asphalt behind, the Lamborghini Huracan Sterrato is sleek, fast, and ready to blaze a trail through the Kauai wilderness.
