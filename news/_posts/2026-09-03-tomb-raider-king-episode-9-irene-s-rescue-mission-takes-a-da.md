Tomb Raider King Episode 9 is here, and this week's installment offers us a combination of relic hunting and comedy, while there is a dangerous mystery around Irene. While this particular episode might not be heavy on action, it did remind us why Joo-Heon is an entertaining main lead to watch in the first place.

It is interesting to see how he stole the relic from the hands of the enemy in the most humiliating way possible by tricking powerful figures. One thing is for sure: Joo-Heon is not interested in playing fair. One of the funniest moments in Tomb Raider King Episode 9 is when he came across an American relic user who boarded the ship.

Initially, it looks like there is going to be an intense battle, but then there is an unexpected turn. Joo-Heon uses his silver axe to attack his opponent and destroys his clothes. He can overpower the enemy in a traditional fight, but it looks like playing mind games is an easier option. This new relic user ends up being extremely embarrassed, which leads him to lose focus.

That is when Joo-Heon used the opening and took advantage of the situation. He then revealed the relic's true form, and it turned out to be an impressive Sword of the Valiant Hwarang Knight, an A-rank epic consumable relic. After successfully stealing it, Joo-Heon simply thanks his opponent and leaves.

This whole sequence represents the personality of the protagonist. He is not someone to care about things like honor or even giving enemies another chance. When there is a chance to gain an easy win to get what he wants, Joo-Heon will not let that opportunity slip by.

In Tomb Raider King Episode 9, we have another important discovery when they find a hidden relic. Joo-Heon cuts through the trees and finds the map of another valuable relic, even though this one is said to have limited uses. The map is important when Irene places her hands on it.

That is when the name of the next destination comes to light, and with that, the protagonist has a potential lead in his attempt to deal with the strange misfortune and dangerous circumstances surrounding Irene. However, rather than handing over the valuable relic, Joo-Heon and his group inTomb Raider King Episode 9 decide to copy it and send away a fake version while keeping the original for themselves.

The fake relic eventually causes trouble when powerful figures realize they have been deceived. The general is particularly unhappy, while Edward confronts Joo-Heon about the missing relic and the deception surrounding the situation.

One of the most entertaining aspects of Tomb Raider King Episode 9 is watching Joo-Heon manipulate nearly every situation around him. He knows how to sound trustworthy even when he is clearly hiding the truth, and he is willing to deceive anyone if it helps him achieve his goals.

Soon, he uses another relic to manipulate the maps in the general's possession, ultimately setting up a scheme that destroys the relic maps he considers dangerous.

The final section of the episode takes Joo-Heon and the others to Turkey, where they enter a tomb connected to Irene's situation. What follows is the biggest shock of Tomb Raider King Episode 9 as Irene begins turning gold. There is a warning that suggests that luck may also attract danger, so it just added another layer of mystery surrounding Irene.

Joo-Heon immediately jumps into the water to save her, but even he appears confused by what is happening. This is particularly interesting because Joo-Heon has seemed completely prepared for nearly every situation so far, but this time he is experiencing a new event.

In the end, with Irene now turning to gold and Joo-Heon facing a situation he may not fully understand, the next episode could finally test whether the always-calculated relic king is truly prepared for everything.
