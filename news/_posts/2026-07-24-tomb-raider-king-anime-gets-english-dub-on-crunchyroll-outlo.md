Crunchyroll has released the Tomb Raider King English dub, making it accessible to a wider English-speaking audience.

Based on SAN.G’s popular web novel and webtoon, Tomb Raider King follows relic raider Jooheon Suh as he travels back in time.

The dub was produced at Crunchyroll’s in-house studio as a part of its broader localization strategy for global anime audiences.

Crunchyroll began streaming the English dub of Tomb Raider King on Wednesday, July 22, giving English-speaking audiences a localized version of the Korean-animated series. The streamer also released the full English cast and production crew list alongside the launch.

The dub release comes roughly two weeks after its original Crunchyroll debut on July 8 with Korean and Japanese audio options. Tomb Raider King now has the English dubbed option for its first episode.

Tomb Raider King follows SAN.G’s web novel and the hit webtoon, illustrated by 3B2S. The narrative centers on relic raider Jooheon Suh’s journey to seize powerful relics and claim the title of Tomb Raider King.

Seung Wook Woo directed the original anime production at Studio EEK, with Hyun-Jung Lee serving as character designer and chief animation director. Ju Young Kim composed the music for the Tomb Raider King anime.

Crunchyroll handled the Tomb Raider King English dubbing at its own studio with Zach Bolton serving as producer. Lee George serves as voice director, and Jonathan Rigg as assistant voice director. Jessica Sluys handled the adaptation into English.
