Key Update: Season 11 kicks off Year 4 on October 7, 2026, introducing a free off-road Kauai map, 21 new vehicles including the Bugatti Tourbillon, and a paid Tomb Raider crossover DLC.

Quick Answer: The Crew Motorfest Season 11 releases October 7, 2026, featuring a free off-road Kauai expansion, 21 new vehicles, winches, and a Tomb Raider DLC.

Season 11 will kick off Year 4 for The Crew Motorfest. It is shaping up to be another solid year as Ivory Tower continues the open-world racer's excellent post-launch run.

The update headlines include a brand-new off-road map, 21 new vehicles including the Bugatti Tourbillon, and much more.

Continue reading to learn everything coming to The Crew Motorfest with Season 11.

Season 11 of The Crew Motorfest will release on October 7, 2026.

The biggest new addition to the open world for Season 11 is a free, brand-new island, Kauai.

It's themed entirely around off-road exploration that has shades of SnowRunner and Expeditions: A Mud Runner Game.

Swamps, canyons, hidden tunnels, dense jungles, beaches, trails, and pretty much everything you can imagine for an untamed wilderness are there in Kauai.

Be careful where you tread, however, as your vehicles will struggle in places like dense swamps.

A compass and winch are two things that you'll be using a lot on the map. Much of the island is unmarked on the map, and everything is for you to discover.

A pop-up does alert you when you're closer to certain activities like Beacon and photographs. You can use your compass bar at the top to check close-by activities.

Speaking of activities, you'll find these scattered across the new expansion:

Eight main events Swamp Escape Jungle Extraction Green Dive Storm Valley Red Charge Sky Scouting Blue Pulse Kauai Grande Adventure

Plenty of these will require you to use a winch in a staple off-road fashion.

Activities like Beacons also require a winch. Not all vehicles will have the feature, of course. Some specific off-road vehicles are designed for it.

Season 11 of The Crew Motorfest will add 21 cars. 17 of the 21 cars have been either confirmed or leaked.

The Suzuki Jimny 2018 Rally Raid is the reward for completing the expansion's main playlist.

Kauai isn't the only expansion coming with Season 11. A brand-new premium crossover with Tomb Raider will bring its own short new region and activities.

Unlike Kauai, the crossover is paid, requiring you to either purchase it separately or buy the Year 4 Pass. The DLC does appear to feature substantial content.

You'll be solving Tomb Raider-style puzzles and have a giant T-Rex chase you, among other new things.

Lara Croft will guide you through everything, including these activities:

Eight main events The Forbidden Island (available in demo) The Lost Land (available in demo) Sacred Fire Swamped Puzzle Surrounded The Temple of La’Amaomao The Goddess’ Wrath Time to Run

While free players won't see the sight of T-Rex, the update will add wildlife across the open world.
