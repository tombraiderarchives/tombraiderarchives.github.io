Tomb Raider turns 30 on October 24, and Crystal Dynamics is marking the anniversary with collaborations across games, comics, collectibles, and gaming hardware. The lineup includes a The Crew Motorfest crossover, a G.I. JOE comic series, and two upcoming 8BitDo controllers.

The Crew Motorfest x Tomb Raider collaboration brings Lara and Jonah into a PvE adventure featuring exploration, environmental puzzles, ancient artifacts, and a T-Rex encounter. The crossover also includes five exclusive vehicles.

Lara is teaming up with another familiar franchise in G.I. JOE: A Real American Hero x Tomb Raider. The first issue is available now from Skybound and Image Comics, kicking off a five-issue limited series.

Fanbrix has announced an officially licensed Croft Manor brick set with 4,355 pieces. The set includes characters, vehicles, and details drawn from the series’ history.

Limited Run Games also has a Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration Collector’s Edition for Nintendo Switch 2. The package includes the full game on cartridge, a Steelcase, a Trinity patch, and a light-up Divine Source replica desk ornament.

8BitDo will release two special-edition Tomb Raider controllers on October 24, coinciding with the anniversary.

On October 27, Dark Horse will release Chronicles of the Tomb Raider, a 184-page hardcover covering Lara’s adventures in chronological order. The book examines the characters, artifacts, and locations that have shaped the series over the past three decades.
