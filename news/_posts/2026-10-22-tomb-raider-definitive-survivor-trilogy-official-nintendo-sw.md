Check out the Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy Nintendo Switch 2 launch trailer for this collection featuring three action games that dive into Lara Croft's full origin story. The Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy bundle includes Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration, and Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, as well as the online modes and all DLC released for each title.

Follow Lara on her formative journey across the world, starting in Tomb Raider where she finds herself trapped on the shrouded island of Yamatai off the coast of Japan, to the harsh Siberian tundra hiding an immortal secret in Rise of the Tomb Raider, and finally to the mountainous landscape of Peru to uncover a cataclysmic power in Shadow of the Tomb Raider.

Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy is available now on Nintendo Switch 2.
