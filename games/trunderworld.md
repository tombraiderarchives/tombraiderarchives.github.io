The conclusion of the Legend Era trilogy, Underworld follows Lara as she searches for the Norse underworld of Niflheim and the mythical Mjölnir — Thor's hammer — while uncovering the truth about her mother's fate. The story directly continues threads from both Legend and Anniversary.

Underworld was praised for its ambitious scope and improved physics, though it received a mixed reception due to some technical issues. It marked the end of Crystal Dynamics' first run with the character before the franchise was rebooted with the Survivor Era trilogy.
