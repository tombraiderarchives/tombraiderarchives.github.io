The second entry in the Survivor Era, Rise sees a more confident Lara pursuing the mythical city of Kitezh — the lost city of immortality — in Siberia, while being hunted by Trinity, the shadowy paramilitary organisation responsible for her father's downfall.

The game significantly expanded on the systems of the 2013 reboot, adding deeper crafting, more elaborate optional tombs, and a stronger focus on exploration. It was widely praised as an improvement over its predecessor, with richer environments and a more capable Lara. Originally an Xbox console exclusive, it later came to PlayStation 4 and PC.
