You may have recently caught Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition in Limited Run Games’ legendary Vault, a place where beloved retro games have another chance to be purchased in physical form for a keen and willing set of players…but only in limited numbers. Such is the popularity of Tomb Raider’s bold adventurer, Lara Croft, that Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition sold out in record time, leaving many fans eager for another chance to add it to their collections.

Well…sometimes, something is so important that it means that a rule, once set in stone, needs to be broken. Limited Run Games listened intently to the requests of these passionate Tomb Raider fans, and ran a poll, asking if fans would be willing to see this longstanding rule be stretched a little, and for Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition to be opened up for another round of pre-orders. The overwhelming response to this was YES, so here it is - fans now have another opportunity to complete the Tomb Raider Trilogy with all three titles available: Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition; Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration; and Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition.

As was mentioned, re-releases from Limited Run Games are extremely rare, so make sure you take advantage of this opportunity while you can. It may not ever happen again. As a special bonus for lovers of the games, anyone who orders Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition will receive a free slipcase that will encase the entire trilogy in one neat package.

Pre-orders for Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition will now be available on the Limited Ruin Games store until an extended date of December 6, 2026, at 23:59 PM EST, for USD $49.99 and you can find it here.
