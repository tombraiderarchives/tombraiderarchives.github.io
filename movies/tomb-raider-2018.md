A reboot of the film franchise drawing heavily from the 2013 video game. Seven years after the disappearance of her father Lord Richard Croft, a twenty-one-year-old Lara is working as a London bicycle courier. A cryptic message leads her to her father's research into Himiko — the "Death Queen" of Yamatai — and to an island in the Devil's Sea where a ruthless Trinity operative named Mathias Vogel has been searching for her father's secrets.

Alicia Vikander earned praise for her grounded, physical performance. The film was a moderate box-office success and a sequel was planned, though it remains in development limbo.
