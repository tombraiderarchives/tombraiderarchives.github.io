**Tomb Raider I–III Remastered** is out today on Windows, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch. Developed by Aspyr Media, the collection brings all three original Core Design games to modern platforms with rebuilt visuals and quality-of-life improvements.

Key features of the remaster include:

- **Toggle between classic and remastered graphics** at any time using a single button press.
- **Optional modern controls** alongside the original tank controls.
- **Full achievement and trophy support** on all platforms.
- **All original levels and secrets preserved**, including bonus levels and expansion content from the original PC releases.

The collection is rated Mature and is available on digital storefronts. Physical editions are also available through selected retailers.
