Tomb Raider turns 30 in 2026, and although the games developed by Amazon Games are nowhere near ready in time for the celebrations, we’ll have some accessories that are more than ready to celebrate Lara Croft’s legacy. In collaboration with Gunnar, the Tomb Raider Croft Glasses have been announced, bringing a classic element of the franchise into the real world.

The glasses, which feature the classic round shape that Lara wore in her early games, incorporate Gunnar’s well-known G-Shield blue light reduction technology, along with an anti-reflective coating and smudge-resistant finish. Their hinges are also flexible to enhance comfort. The frame is made of stainless steel and features interesting details throughout. The temple features a stylized “C,” representing the Croft family, while the frame itself has small embossed patterns. The temple tip, meanwhile, bears the Tomb Raider logo.

The glasses will be available starting on July 15 at a price starting at $99 for the Amber, Amber Max, and Sun models, and at $135 for the Amber Sun Shift model.

For now, Tomb Raider has two titles on the horizon. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is a remake of Lara Croft’s first adventure, set to arrive in February 2027. Meanwhile, Tomb Raider: Catalyst is scheduled to arrive sometime else in 2027. As if that weren’t enough, Tomb Raider will also make the leap to the small screen with an adaptation starring Sophie Turner, coming to Prime Video.
