Lara Croft's epic adventure is being reimagined with Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, and the game has already gained a large following of excited prospective players. After having been announced in December 2025, there has been an increasing buzz across the internet of people anticipating not only what the game will be, but when it will be released.

This is actually one of two Tomb Raider games in development, with Tomb Raider: Catalyst continuing Lara's story as a brand-new entry in the overarching narrative of the franchise. While we know that Catalyst is expected to launch sometime in 2027, Legacy of Atlantis had previously been said to be coming first. Therefore, not only does Legacy of Atlantis take on the massive responsibility of re-imagining the original Tomb Raider for the second time, but it also could impact anticipation for Catalyst.

Despite that pressure on the game's developers, what fans want to know is simply when they can play it. Previously, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis had been given a release window for "Coming 2026" with nothing else to narrow it down.
