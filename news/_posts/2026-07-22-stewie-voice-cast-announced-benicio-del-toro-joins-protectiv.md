The voice cast has been set for the upcoming Family Guy spinoff series Stewie. Joining the series are Kenan Thompson, Vanessa Bayer, Mike Henry, Jessica Lowe, Melissa O’Neil, and Aaron Lee. They join Seth MacFarlane.

Benicio Del Toro is set to star in comedy series Protective Custody on Apple TV. Also starring and executive produced by Ben Stiller, and hailing from Mike Judge, Steve Hely and Dave King, comes the series about a disgraced financier navigating life after arrest.

The English dub of Tomb Raider King premieres July 22 on Crunchyroll. Chaos ensues overnight when mysterious tombs suddenly surface, filled with divine relics granting overwhelming power, triggering a global power struggle to claim them for dominion. Among the havoc, a raider, Jooheon Suh, is betrayed and left for dead until a second chance brings him back years before the relics. Fueled by fury and future knowledge, he sets out to raid each tomb before they arise.

Let’s Marry Harry premieres on Netflix August 5, with the finale set to air August 12. Heartthrob Harry Jowsey is done playing the field. This time, he’s handing the reins of his love life to those who know him best. After years of dating in the spotlight – and getting it wrong – his closest confidants Amanda Kloots, Georgia Hassarati and Sonny Henty are stepping in to make the tough calls. From a carefully selected pool of potential matches, they’ll decide who stays and who goes, guiding Harry toward what he truly wants: authentic love and marriage.

Reid Scott will be departing Law & Order after three seasons. Scott’s character will get a sendoff as he will appear in episodes of the 26th season. James Badge Dale will then join the series as a senior detective.

Anime film Assassination Classroom the Movie: Our Time begins streaming on Crunchyroll July 23. The film is a part of the 10th anniversary of the series. Forget about homework and pop quizzes. The students of Class 3E have a far more important assignment: kill their teacher before the end of the year! A tentacle-d sensei that moves at Mach 20 is out to conquer the classroom after destroying seventy percent of the moon. In addition to reading, writing, and arithmetic, the murderous monster behind the lectern will teach his students everything he knows about the assassination game. Should some eager beaver prove to be a quick study in killing, he or she will save Earth from extinction – and collect a hefty reward.

Paramount+ has ordered Scottish Highland drama series Laird. Set in the modern Scottish Highlands, the series is an epic family saga in which a proud Scot defies a shadowy American billionaire hellbent on buying up the glens. Amidst family feuds, buried secrets and star-crossed romances, the Laird of Castle Drummoch (Kenneth Branagh) remains determined to protect his ancestral lands from oligarchical land grabbing.

Apple TV has landed the rights to Rebecca Yarros’ forthcoming novel “Peculiar Stars.” Apple Studios will develop the series, and Yarros will serve as executive producer through her banner Full Measures Productions.

BritBox has set premiere dates for upcoming drama series and more. Season 46 of Antiques Roadshow premieres August 1. The Hardacres premieres August 5. Season four of I Literally Just Told You premieres August 10. Season 14 of 8 Out of 10 Cats Does Countdown premieres August 10. Season eight of Pointless Celebrities UK premieres August 10. Prey premieres August 13. Vanity Fair premieres August 19. Season three of The Good Ship Murder premieres August 25.

Investigation Discovery’s three-part docuseries Unmasking a Monster: Aileen Wuornos will premiere September 30. The series uses advanced digital replication and VFX techniques to revisit the life, crimes, and eventual capture of Aileen Wuornos.
