Treasures of Tomb Raider have been put on display to mark the 30 year anniversary of the birth of Lara Croft.

At a studio at 55 Ashbourne Road, Derby, back in the 1990s game designers created the archaeologist-adventurer who would go on to conquer the world.

Now Pickford's House in Derby, just down the road, has been transformed into her home Croft Manor to honour one of the city's most famous creations.

The free Lara Croft: 30 Years On exhibition features collectibles, costumes and rare artefacts including the computer used by Core Design to bring the legend to life.
