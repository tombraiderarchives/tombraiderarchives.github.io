As per the Black Friday listing, games such as God of War and GoW Ragnarok will be available at half price. TEKKEN 8 - Advanced Edition is listed at 45 per cent off, compared to its MRP. Further, the GTA 5 and RDR lineup is also available at massive discounts.

Here are a few picks from the list of top "best-selling" gaming titles that are available at massive discounts right now.
