<div class="page-hero">
  <h1>Timeline</h1>
  <p>A chronological history of the Tomb Raider franchise, from Lara Croft's creation in 1993 to the present day.</p>
</div>

<div class="timeline">

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">1993</p>
    <p class="timeline-title">Lara Croft Conceived <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Development</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Toby Gard begins developing Lara Croft at Core Design in Derby, UK. Originally conceived as a Brazilian character named Laura Cruz, she is later reworked into the British aristocrat Lara Croft to avoid clichés.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">October 25, 1996</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr1/">Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The original Tomb Raider launches on Sega Saturn in Europe, followed by PC and PlayStation releases. Developed by Core Design and published by Eidos Interactive, it introduces Lara Croft to the world and becomes a massive commercial and critical success.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">October 31, 1997</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr2/">Tomb Raider II</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The sequel expands Lara's adventures to Venice, a sunken ocean liner, and the Great Wall of China. It introduces new vehicles, a wider arsenal, and the iconic Venice boat chase sequence. Sells over eight million copies worldwide.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">November 21, 1998</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr3/">Tomb Raider III</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The most ambitious classic-era entry introduces a non-linear level order — after the Indian prologue, players choose their next destination from the South Pacific, Nevada, or London. Known for its notoriously high difficulty.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">October 29, 1999</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr4/">Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Core Design intended this as the final game in the series. Set almost entirely in Egypt, the story chronicles a young Lara accidentally releasing the Egyptian god Seth. The ending — Lara apparently perishing under a collapsing temple — shocked players worldwide.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">November 24, 2000</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr5/">Tomb Raider: Chronicles</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">An anthology told through flashbacks as Lara's friends mourn her disappearance after the events of The Last Revelation. The four separate storylines revisit Rome, a Russian submarine, and a high-tech skyscraper. Developed quickly and received a mixed critical response.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">June 15, 2001</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/lara-croft-tomb-raider/">Lara Croft: Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--film">Film</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Angelina Jolie portrays Lara Croft in the first live-action film. The movie grosses over $274 million worldwide despite mixed critical reviews, making it one of the most successful video game film adaptations of its era.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">June 20, 2003</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr6/">Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Released in troubled circumstances following an extended development, Angel of Darkness takes Lara to Paris and Prague in a darker, narrative-heavy story. Technical issues and design compromises led to poor reviews. Core Design loses the franchise shortly after release.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">August 15, 2003</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/cradle-of-life/">Lara Croft: Tomb Raider — The Cradle of Life</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--film">Film</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The sequel starring Angelina Jolie centres on the search for Pandora's Box. Directed by Jan de Bont, it performs below the original at the box office and no further films with Jolie are made.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">April 7, 2006</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/trlegend/">Tomb Raider: Legend</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Crystal Dynamics takes over the franchise from Core Design and relaunches Lara with a new, modern design and fluid gameplay. Keeley Hawes voices Lara for the first time. The Legend–Anniversary–Underworld trilogy begins here, rebooting Lara's continuity.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">June 1, 2007</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tranniversary/">Tomb Raider: Anniversary</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">A full remake of the 1996 original, built on the Legend engine, released to celebrate the franchise's 10th anniversary. Widely praised for faithfully recreating the original's environments while modernising the controls and visual style.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">November 18, 2008</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/trunderworld/">Tomb Raider: Underworld</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The conclusion of Crystal Dynamics' Legend trilogy sends Lara to the bottom of the Mediterranean, Thailand, and the Arctic. Despite strong visuals and gameplay, lower-than-expected sales prompt Square Enix to direct the franchise toward a full reboot.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">March 5, 2013</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr2013/">Tomb Raider (2013)</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">A full origin-story reboot. A young Lara Croft is shipwrecked on the mythical island of Yamatai, fighting to survive and rescue her crew. Developed by Crystal Dynamics with Rhianna Pratchett writing the screenplay and Camilla Luddington voicing Lara. Sells over four million copies in its first month.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">November 10, 2015</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/rottr/">Rise of the Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The Survivor trilogy continues with Lara searching for the legendary city of Kitezh in Siberia. Initially a timed Xbox exclusive, it later releases on PlayStation 4 and PC. Praised for its expanded tombs, crafting system, and more confident characterisation of Lara.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">March 16, 2018</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/tomb-raider-2018/">Tomb Raider (2018)</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--film">Film</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">A cinematic reboot loosely based on the 2013 game, starring Alicia Vikander as a young Lara Croft. Directed by Roar Uthaug, the film earns a mixed but generally more positive reception than its predecessors.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">September 14, 2018</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/sottr/">Shadow of the Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The conclusion of the Survivor trilogy. Developed by Eidos Montréal, with Crystal Dynamics in a supporting role. Set in Peru and a hidden Mayan city called Paititi, it challenges Lara with stealth, jungle survival, and the consequences of her recklessness.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">February 14, 2024</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tomb-raider-remastered-01/">Tomb Raider I–III Remastered</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Aspyr Media releases modernised versions of the first three Core Design games for contemporary platforms. Players can toggle between classic and remastered graphics at any time. The collection preserves all original levels and introduces achievement support.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">October 10, 2024</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/legend-of-lara-croft/">The Legend of Lara Croft</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--series">Series</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">An animated series produced for Netflix, set after the events of Shadow of the Tomb Raider. Lara seeks a stolen artefact that threatens to destabilise Europe. Maintains continuity with the Survivor trilogy.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <p class="timeline-year">2025</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tomb-raider-remastered-04/">Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Aspyr Media follows up the first remaster collection with restored versions of The Last Revelation, Chronicles, and The Angel of Darkness, bringing the full classic Core Design catalogue to modern platforms.</p>
  </div>

</div>
