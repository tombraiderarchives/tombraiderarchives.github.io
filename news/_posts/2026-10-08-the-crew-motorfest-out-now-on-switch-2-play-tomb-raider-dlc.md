The Crew Motorfest is now available on Nintendo Switch™ 2, bringing the newly released Season 11: Expeditions, the game’s most ambitious and expansive content yet, to Nintendo players. The Nintendo Switch™ 2 version has been carefully optimized to preserve the richness of Motorfest’s experience while embracing the freedom of playing anywhere, anytime.

New features, including wildlife roaming across the archipelago

The update is available across all platforms, including Switch 2.

In Season 11, The Crew Motorfest partnered with Crystal Dynamics for a premium DLC pack including exclusive vehicles, a new island, and a brand-new playlist, Tomb Raider: Secrets of Ni'ihau. Players will join Lara Croft as she searches for her missing friend who vanished after chasing a powerful relic. Using Lara’s guidance from afar (Alix Wilton Regan, the new official voice of Lara Croft, will endorse the English Voice Over), players will navigate a storm-lashed jungle crawling with ancient ruins and deadly predators as every mile brings them closer to the truth.

The Crew Motorfest's map also expands this season with the new free island Kauai. Players can experience this new addition via the Kauai Expeditions Playlist and carve the first roads across its coast and cliffs. The free playlist features eight main events, 40 animals to photograph, and the new Road Discovery feature, which encourages players to travel along all the exciting paths in the game to explore the world, uncover the map, and earn rewards.

In addition to two new playlists, Season 11 includes a new feature: winch & towing. Available on select vehicles, the winch turns every dead end into an opportunity: haul debris, crack puzzles, explore new blaze trails, and even drag other vehicles out of trouble. To take on these towing challenges, there's a selection of vehicles that are more than up to the task: the Hyundai Crater Concept, Suzuki Jimny Winch Ed, and the Mercedes-Benz G 63 AMG 6x6. Season 11 also includes the Lamborghini Huracan Sterrato. Built to leave the asphalt behind, the Lamborghini Huracan Sterrato is sleek, fast, and ready to blaze a trail through the Kauai wilderness.

Players can also experience a new Special Event, including a showstopper dedicated entirely to the legendary Bugatti Tourbillon, a new Story dropping mid-season, and a brand-new Trackforge Kit built for off-road glory, taking community creations further than ever.
