Originally announced for release in 2026, Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog’s remake of the original Tomb Raider, titled Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, is a game I have been greatly anticipating this year. However, when the release date was revealed during yesterday’s PlayStation State of Play, I was surprised to see that the game had been delayed. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will now release on February 12, 2027.

It’s not all bad news! The new trailer that provided this date reveal gave us our best look at the game yet.

In Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, players will step into the boots of Lara Croft as she embarks on a treacherous globe-trotting quest to discover the lost secrets of Atlantis, with jaw-dropping visuals, modernized gameplay, and new surprises that honor the spirit of the original game. Immersive environments, familiar puzzles redefined with new mechanics, and unforgettable moments – like the legendary battle against the T-Rex...
