Embark on a riveting journey in Shadow of the Tomb Raider where Lara Croft faces her defining moment as she races to save the world from a Mayan apocalypse she may have unleashed. Set in dense jungles and ancient ruins Lara must challenge Trinity confront her mistakes and uncover the secrets of a lost civilization.

With expanded traversal stealth and combat systems Lara evolves into the Tomb Raider she was destined to become. Developed by Crystal Dynamics and Eidos Montreal this atmospheric and cinematic adventure concludes Lara's origin trilogy.
