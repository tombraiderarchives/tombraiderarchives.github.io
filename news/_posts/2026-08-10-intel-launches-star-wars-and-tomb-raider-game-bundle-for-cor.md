Intel has launched its new Gamer Days Bundle promotion, offering two games with qualifying Intel processors, graphics products and systems. The promotion starts August 10 and may run through September 13, depending on the participating retailer.

The bundle includes STAR WARS: Galactic Racer and Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis. Intel assigns an average retail value of $59.99 to each game, putting the combined value at $119.98. Both are provided as full game downloads at no additional cost with an eligible purchase.

Intel has included a long list of hardware in the promotion. This covers Arc G3 graphics, Core 200H laptop processors, 13th and 14th Gen Core chips, Core Ultra 100 and 200 series processors, newer Core Ultra Plus models and Core Ultra 300 H-series products. Eligibility can still vary by retailer, so not every listed processor will necessarily qualify at every store.

Intel Core 200H: Core 5 210H/220H, Core 7 230H/240H/250H, Core 9 270H

Intel Core Ultra 200: Core Ultra 5 225/235/245 series, Core Ultra 7 251HX/255H/255HX/258V/265 series, Core Ultra 9 275HX/285 series

Intel Core Ultra Plus: Core Ultra 5 250K Plus/250KF Plus, Core Ultra 7 270K Plus/270HX Plus, Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus

Intel Core Ultra 300 H: Core Ultra 5 336H/338H, Core Ultra 7 356H/366H, Core Ultra 9 386H

Intel also lists several suffix variants separately, including F, K, KF, T, H and HX models. The full promotion list therefore covers both boxed desktop processors and systems using qualifying mobile CPUs. Intel notes that eligible products can include processors, processor-based devices, graphics cards or complete systems, depending on the seller. There’s even a separate mention of MSI handhled with Lunar Lake SOC.

Customers can redeem the bundle until October 31, 2026. Depending on the retailer, Intel may require a Master Key, a hardware scan or purchase verification with proof of purchase. Each qualifying product can receive only one offer, and returned or canceled purchases are not eligible.
