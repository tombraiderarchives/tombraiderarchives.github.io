Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, the reinterpretation of the 1996 original, will arrive on February 12, 2027, on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and Steam. The saga celebrates its 30th anniversary following the surprise release of Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition on Nintendo's hybrid console, a move that reinforces the franchise's presence on the platform.

An update to the Amazon listing published on November 9, 2026, suggests that Tomb Raider: Catalyst, the new Crystal Dynamics title planned for 2028, will also be released on Nintendo's hybrid console. Although there is no official announcement, the modified text in the product listing mentions the platform alongside the home console versions. Confirmation of Catalyst on Switch 2 would strengthen the console's action and adventure catalog for 2028, a year in which Nintendo needs to sustain the pace of third-party releases.

We already know how this works: companies stay silent, rumors fill the void, and in the end an Amazon listing in a hurry to publish uncovers the announcement before any press release. If the pattern repeats, in 2028 we will see Lara Croft jumping between the deals and personalized recommendations sections. In the meantime, we must wait for official confirmation, although the shopping cart already seems to know more than the studios themselves.
