Part of the Tomb Raider I–III Remastered collection, this is the definitive version of the 1996 original. Aspyr rebuilt the game's visuals with high-resolution textures, modern lighting, and improved character models — while keeping the level geometry and gameplay 100% faithful to the original.

Players can toggle between the new remastered visuals and the original 1996 graphics at any time. Modern control schemes are available alongside the classic tank controls. The remaster includes achievement support across all platforms and represents the most accessible way to experience the game that launched the franchise.
