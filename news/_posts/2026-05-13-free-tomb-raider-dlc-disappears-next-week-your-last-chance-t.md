Tomb Raider fans have only a limited time to pick up a free DLC before it’s gone forever.

With the Tomb Raider franchise celebrating its 30th anniversary this year, multiple new games, tie-in products, and cross-promotions have been released.

While we wait for more Tomb Raider releases in the near future, players have a limited time to act in picking up this free DLC which will be removed from storefronts next week.

As FuturLab announced earlier this year, the Tomb Raider Special Pack for PowerWash Simulator will be delisted from storefronts on 19 May, 2026.

Similarly, the same will be happening with the Final Fantasy 7-themed DLC, the Midgar Special Pack.

Both DLCs are free, and only require you to own the game on your platform of choice to be able to redeem them. After 19 May, they’ll no longer be available as part of PowerWash Simulator’s DLC offerings.

The reason for this removal would most likely be down to expiring licences. FuturLab will have been able to license the Tomb Raider IP for a limited time to be able to develop this content.

However, once that expires, the content must be taken down from storefronts. Users who have already downloaded it can continue to keep playing, though.

The decision is also tied to the end of FuturLab’s partnership with Square Enix, which published the first PowerWash Simulator game.

The Tomb Raider Special Pack for PowerWash Simulator presents the player with a big mess to clean up—Croft Manor.

“Whilst some seek the thrill of adventure, others prefer good clean fun - that’s where you come in!” reads the DLC’s description. “Grapple with grime in the ultimate quest to defeat all dirt covering Croft Manor and its grounds, along with Lara’s trusty vehicles.

“Lara’s childhood home is impressive but in need of some serious TLC to restore it to its full potential - rumour has it, the owner much prefers a life of danger to domesticity. You’ve been commissioned to undertake the ultimate cleaning caper.

“Your client may be used to less-than-ideal conditions out on the trail, but only the very best will do when it comes to the Croft residence. Unwind and blast away every speck of dirt you can track down, whilst Lara regales you with tales of past adventures.”

You can continue to pick up the Tomb Raider Special Pack for PowerWash Simulator until next Tuesday, 19 May 2026.

Crystal Dynamics is set to release Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis later this year on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.

The game is a ground-up remake of the original 1996 game, with a new 'unified' Lara Croft acting as a combination of every different era of the character so far.

There's no release date for Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, but rumours suggest that an appearance at Summer Game Fest will reveal the release date.
