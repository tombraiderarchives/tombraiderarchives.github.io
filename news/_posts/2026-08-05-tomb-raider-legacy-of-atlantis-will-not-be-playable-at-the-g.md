There’s some bad news for Lara Croft fans. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis won't be playable anytime soon.

In a post on social media, Crystal Dynamics confirmed that there will be no playable demo of the remake of the first game at Gamescom 2026. The German trade show will take place August 26–30 in Cologne.

However, that doesn’t mean the game will be completely absent. A new trailer could appear there. As a reminder, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will be released on the PS5, XBOX Series, Switch 2, and PC on February 12, 2027.
