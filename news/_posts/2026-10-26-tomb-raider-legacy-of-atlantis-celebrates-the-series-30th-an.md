The Tomb Raider series has just celebrated its 30th anniversary. Released on October 24, 1996, the first game in the franchise is getting a remake next year with Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis.

To mark the occasion, Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog have released a new trailer. It offers a fresh look at the gameplay for the title ahead of its release on consoles and PC on Februar 12, 2027.

It’s worth noting that a completely new installment titled Tomb Raider: Catalyst is scheduled for release in 2028.
