While originally announced for 2026, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis may keep us waiting a little longer. Earlier this month, we already got rumors about a possible delay of the remake until next year, and those are now gaining further traction.

Several content creators citing "a trusted source" said to be close to Crystal Dynamics or Flying Wild Hog (the support studio assisting the project). A new trailer could be released during the Summer Game Fest in June to confirm a new release date for February 12, 2027.

Thus, Tomb Raider: Catalyst, which is the next brand-new entry in the saga, could end up pushed back to 2028.
