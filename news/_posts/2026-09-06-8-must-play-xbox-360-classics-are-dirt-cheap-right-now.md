If you’re looking to play some classic Xbox 360 games on Xbox Series X/S, there are plenty of bargains to grab in the latest Xbox Marketplace sale. Better yet, fans of the Tomb Raider series can pick up several beloved classics at heavily discounted prices, while one of the most iconic GTA games is also available for a seriously tempting price.

As much as we love the joys that modern video games can bring, from breathtaking visuals and deep gameplay mechanics to compelling stories, sometimes there’s nothing better than taking a trip down memory lane. If that sounds like you, Xbox consoles offer arguably the best way to revisit classic games, with Microsoft’s approach to backwards compatibility giving it an edge over rivals such as PlayStation and Nintendo.

Since the launch of the Xbox 360 in 2005, Microsoft has embraced backwards compatibility, initially allowing players to enjoy a selection of original Xbox games on its newer console. That commitment has continued to evolve with each generation, expanding through the Xbox One and, more recently, the Xbox Series X/S, which can play a huge selection of classic Xbox and Xbox 360 games.

Right now, there are loads of games heavily discounted on the Xbox Marketplace, with Xbox 360 fans particularly well catered for. If you’re a fan of classic series such as Tomb Raider and GTA, there are plenty of great bargains to be found, so we’ve rounded up some of the best deals you can grab right now.

With that in mind, let’s take a look at some of the best Xbox 360 bargains available.

Play one of the best entries in Rockstar Games’ Grand Theft Auto series with GTA IV, which boasts an impressive 97 Metacritic score. You play as Niko Bellic, an Eastern European immigrant hoping to live the “American Dream” to the fullest, albeit on the wrong side of the law.

Set in the gritty Liberty City, GTA IV has aged remarkably well thanks to its exploration, compelling story, action, and wealth of side activities. Well, apart from Niko’s annoying cousin Roman and his seemingly endless calls asking you to go bowling. The deal ends on September 10.

This underrated tower defence game is packed with strong Command & Conquer vibes. Unlike most games in the genre, Anomaly: Warzone Earth offers a surprising amount of strategic depth and resource management as you fight to repel a relentless alien invasion. Best of all, you can grab it at a discount right now, with the deal ending on September 10.

If you love proper old-school retro games, Midway Arcade Origins is well worth a look. It features more than 30 classic titles, including Rampage, Gauntlet and Smash TV, giving you a fantastic selection of arcade favourites in one package. At this price, it’s an absolute steal, but you’ll need to act fast as the deal ends on September 9.

Before Cyberpunk 2077, there was the Deus Ex series, and Human Revolution remains one of its very best entries. Combining high-octane action, a compelling story and deep augmentation upgrades with a sinister sci-fi conspiracy, it’s easy to see why this game remains so beloved, and why protagonist Adam Jensen is such a badass. The deal ends on September 11.

If you relish utter destruction, there’s a good chance you’ll love the Red Faction series. Red Faction: Armageddon was the last game released in the series, and it might just be the best of the bunch. This third-person action shooter takes the destruction to Mars, where you play as Darius Mason, who must battle a sinister alien threat.

With plenty of over-the-top destruction and ridiculous fun, Red Faction: Armageddon delivers exactly the kind of chaotic action you’d expect from the series. Oddly, the Xbox Store page doesn’t mention when this deal expires, so hopefully it’s still available when you read this.
