Tomb Raider King has officially begun, with the new webtoon adaptation bringing its relic-hunting fantasy to Crunchyroll, and the release dates of its extra episodes after the premiere coming up soon, as well.

Based on the original work by 3B2S, Yuns of REDICE STUDIO and SAN.G, the anime is being made by Studio EEK, with Woo Seung Wook serving as both director and series composer.

Like its source material, it follows tomb raider Ryoga Goriki as a mishap finds him in the distant past.

Now that episode 1 has arrived, read on for everything you need to know about the Tomb Raider King release schedule, so you’ll know when all other episodes will be dropping.

If the rest of the series follows this pattern and there are no interruptions, the remaining Tomb Raider King episodes in its release schedule will be out on the following dates – although note that this is always subject to change:

Tomb Raider King episode 1 – Wednesday 8 July 2026 (out now)

Tomb Raider King is set in a world where mysterious tombs appear across the globe, each containing relics that can grant supernatural abilities to their owners. Those relics quickly become the centre of a dangerous power struggle, with treasure hunters and powerful organisations racing to claim them.

Ryoga Goriki is one of the people making a living from those tombs, but his life changes when he is betrayed and left on the edge of death. After being saved by a mysterious crow, he wakes up 15 years in the past, giving him the chance to use everything he knows to change his fate.

With a second chance in front of him, Ryoga sets out to claim the relics before anyone else can – and take revenge on the people who destroyed him the first time around.
