Crystal Dynamics has confirmed yet another round of layoffs, marking the fifth time the studio has reduced headcount in less than two years. While only eight employees are affected this time, the latest cuts bring the total number of jobs lost during that period to roughly 70.

In a statement provided to PC Gamer, Crystal Dynamics described the decision as a “necessary business” move and insisted that no ongoing projects would be impacted. The latest cuts also come just eight months after the studio's previous round of redundancies, bringing the total number of employees lost over the past two years to roughly 70.

Crystal Dynamics is currently developing two major Tomb Raider projects, with Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis now targeting a 2027 release and Tomb Raider: Catalyst planned for 2028. While the studio continues to insist that development remains unaffected, the latest cuts come just eight months after the previous round of layoffs and mark the fifth reduction in staff at the studio in less than two years.

Crystal Dynamics spent several years co-developing the Perfect Dark reboot before Microsoft cancelled the project and closed The Initiative in 2025. Since then, the company has undergone multiple rounds of restructuring while simultaneously navigating delays across its two announced Tomb Raider titles. Taken individually, the loss of eight employees is not particularly significant. The latest cuts bring Crystal Dynamics to five rounds of layoffs in less than two years, despite the studio currently working on multiple Tomb Raider projects.
