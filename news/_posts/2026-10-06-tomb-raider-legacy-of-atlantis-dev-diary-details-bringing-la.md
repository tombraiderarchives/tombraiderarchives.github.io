We were always confident Alex Wilton Regan would do a great job as Lara Croft in Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis. However, Crystal Dynamics has surprised us with a new look at Bradley Snedeker as Larson Conway.

If the name sounds familiar but is now too distant to recall, he’s a secondary antagonist who keeps getting in Lara’s way, and sometimes on her nerves as she considers what he calls charm to be a mildly annoying personality. Of course, those of you who played the original release already know how that ends, but we’ll stay mum to avoid wading into spoiler territory. Still, there’s no denying the two characters have solid chemistry and the actors playing them are milking it for all it’s worth.

The team was impressed with Snedeker’s work, as he oozes the kind of overconfident charm that most of us now see as the clarion call of a narcissist. We can’t see ourselves liking Conway as a person, but we’re definitely behind whatever Snedeker’s doing with the character based on the brief glimpse we’ve been given of his work so far. You can see what we mean for yourself below.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is out on February 12th for Xbox Series X/S, PS5, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2.
