One of the most anticipated games of next year is going to be Crystal Dynamics and developer Flying Wild Hog’s Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis. Coming to Nintendo Switch 2, the game is a remake of the original PlayStation/Saturn Tomb Raider game from 1997. While this is technically the second stab at a remake of Lara Croft’s first adventure, Legacy of Atlantis is built from the ground up in Unreal Engine 5 and boasts a reimagined campaign, stunningly realistic visuals, and all-new controls and mechanics.
