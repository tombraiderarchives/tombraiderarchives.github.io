Aspyr is continuing its push to bring over prior Tomb Raider games to Nintendo Switch 2, and today, announced Rise of The Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration for the console. This one is being shadowdropped – it’s out today.

Rise of The Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration packs in the main game and DLC. This includes prior exclusives.

Rise of The Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration is a viscerally intense experience that puts all of Lara’s combat and survival skills to the test, perfect for those who love engaging storytelling, heart-pounding action, customizable loadouts and treacherous environments.

● An Exhilarating Story: Outwit the insidious secret society Trinity and their paramilitary operatives as you race to uncover the Divine Source, hidden deep in the Siberian wilderness and said to harness the power of immortality.

● Challenging Puzzle Tombs: Follow in the footsteps of those before you—the legendary Prophet, the ancient Remnant civilization, and Trinity—outwitting environmental puzzles and ancient traps guarding the relics within.

● Dynamic Combat: Prove you’re worthy of the Croft legacy as you outmaneuver and overcome enemies using scavenged supplies and Lara’s versatile arsenal. Engage in combat or utilize stealth to quickly eliminate Trinity’s forces.

● Survival Upgrades & Customization: Gather resources to craft essential gear and upgrade Lara’s skills. Discover weapon parts and ancient texts to create the specialized tools Lara needs to survive against the relentless powers that stand in her way.

The expedition continues! All DLC and exclusive content are included in this complete edition:

● All Season Pass Content & DLCs: Baba Yaga: The Temple of the Witch, Cold Darkness Awakened, Blood Ties, Lara’s Nightmare, Endurance Mode, and Co-op Endurance

● All Extras: 35 Expansion Cards, Extreme Survivor Difficulty, 5 Classic Lara Skins, and Tomb Raider III Reimagined “Antarctica” Outfit
