"Bears are no joke and you have to move out of their way," says Jason Epps, Design Director at Crystal Dynamics. "Use acrobatics in short chains to get away, and then use focus mode to shoot the rampaging beast in the head."

Where the original bear encounters were about patience, the reimagined version demands movement. Lara can't stand still and trade shots. She has to be acrobatic, reactive, and precise.
