Popular gaming hardware brand 8BitDo will be bringing their Tomb Raider 30th Anniversary 8BitDo Pro 3 Bluetooth Gamepad to players in North America on Oct. 24th and Europe on November 13, 2026.

The 8BitDo Pro 3 Bluetooth Gamepad offers professional control for a wide variety of gaming platforms. Compatible with Nintendo® Switch™ and Nintendo® Switch™2 via Bluetooth, 2.4Ghz or wired USB, the Pro 3 can also connect to Windows™ via 2.4Ghz or wired USB, SteamOS via Bluetooth or wired USB and mobile devices via Bluetooth or wired USB.

TMR powered dual Thumbsticks ensure that movement is sharp and precise, eliminating dreaded stick-drift, while additional R3 and L4 extra bumpers, as well as two pro-level back buttons offer a wealth of configuration options. Hall-effect Triggers combine with a silky-smooth tactile D-Pad meaning that every input on the Pro 3 is built for precision and durability.

Unique to the Pro 3, swappable magnetic ABXY face buttons, allow gamers to switch their inputs for different platform configurations, and when used with Switch or Steam, in-built 6-axis motion control, ensures maximum compatibility with a huge range of titles. Integrated vibration support as well as Turbo function also feature, and once more, the controller includes a stunningly themed Charging Dock for charging and display when not in use.

The Pro 3 controller is fully compatible with the 8BitDo Ultimate Software X and Ultimate Software V2 Mobile software, allowing gamers to endless tweak their controller, for personal preference.
