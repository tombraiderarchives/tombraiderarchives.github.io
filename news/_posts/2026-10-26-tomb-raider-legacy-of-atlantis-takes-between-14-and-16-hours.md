There are still a few months to go before Lara Croft returns to consoles and PC. This comeback will come in the form of the remake Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, scheduled for release on February 12, 2027.

Ahead of that, the website MP1st spoke with Jeff Adams, Director of Experience at Crystal Dynamics, to get some crucial information about the game. It concerns the game’s playtime, which is expected to be comparable to that of the original game. The original game took approximately 12 to 15 hours to complete.

As for Legacy of Atlantis, its story will unfold over 14 to 16 hours, depending on the player’s playstyle.

"I’m comfortable saying we’re somewhere in that 14-to-16-hour range of play, depending on your play style. But what I also like to tell everyone when we talk about this is not to feel rushed. We’ve intentionally built a world that’s meant to draw you in and make you curious to explore, so you don’t have to put a timer on yourself.

Take your time. Take in the immersive ambience the audio team has created. Let that feeling of isolation wash over you, and understand that it’s part of what makes Tomb Raider so special. But yes, I understand we live in a world of spoilers, so all I can recommend is maybe turning off social media for a while and letting yourself enjoy the game. You’ll be rewarded for it."
