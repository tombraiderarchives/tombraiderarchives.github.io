  <h1>Timeline</h1>
  <p>Lara Croft's adventures span three separate, incompatible game continuities. Each section is ordered by the sequence Lara experienced her adventures within that universe — not by real-world release date. The films occupy their own standalone continuities.</p>
</div>

<h2 class="timeline-era-title">Classic Era</h2>
<p class="timeline-era-sub">Core Design &nbsp;·&nbsp; 1996–2003</p>

<div class="timeline">

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr1/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">1996</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr1/">Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara's first adventure begins when arms dealer Jacqueline Natla commissions her to retrieve an ancient artefact called the Scion of Atlantis. The hunt takes her from the jungles of Peru to the ruins of ancient Greece and Egypt, and ultimately to the lost continent of Atlantis itself. The success of this expedition establishes Lara as one of the world's most formidable archaeologists.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr2/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider II cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">1997</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr2/">Tomb Raider II</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara pursues the legendary Dagger of Xian, believed to grant its wielder the power of a dragon. Her investigation takes her to the Great Wall of China, the canals of Venice, a sunken ocean liner in the South China Sea, and the mountains of Tibet — where she confronts the ruthless crime lord Marco Bartoli and his cult of Fiamma Nera.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr3/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider III cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">1998</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr3/">Tomb Raider III</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Beginning in the jungles of India, Lara searches for four artefacts carved from a meteorite that crashed in Antarctica millennia ago. Players can tackle the middle destinations — the South Pacific, Nevada desert, and London — in any order before the trail leads to an Antarctic research base where the meteorite's true, horrifying nature is revealed.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr4/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">1999</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr4/">Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">The adventure opens with a flashback to 1984 — a teenage Lara on her first expedition with archaeologist Werner Von Croy in Cambodia. In the present day, Lara inadvertently breaks the seal on the tomb of the Egyptian god Seth, unleashing a catastrophe across Egypt. Racing to undo the damage, she faces Von Croy one final time before being seemingly trapped beneath a collapsing temple.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr5/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Chronicles cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">2000</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr5/">Tomb Raider: Chronicles</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Believing Lara to be dead after the events in Egypt, her closest friends gather at Croft Manor to share memories of her life. Their stories unfold as four playable episodes: a teenage Lara stealing a powerful artefact from a gangster in Rome; a mission on a Cold War-era Russian submarine; and an infiltration of a heavily-guarded skyscraper in New York. The framing story is set in the aftermath of The Last Revelation.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr6/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">2003</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr6/">Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara resurfaces in Paris, wanted for the murder of her former mentor Werner Von Croy. Investigating a series of killings tied to an ancient alchemist named the Alchemist and a shadowy organisation called the Cabal, she crosses paths with a new ally — mercenary Kurtis Trent — across Paris and Prague. Released after a troubled development, it was Core Design's final Tomb Raider game.</p>
  </div>

</div>

<h2 class="timeline-era-title">Legend / Anniversary Era</h2>
<p class="timeline-era-sub">Crystal Dynamics &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2006–2008 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Entries ordered by Lara's in-universe experience, not release date</p>

<div class="timeline">

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tranniversary/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Anniversary cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">First adventure</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tranniversary/">Tomb Raider: Anniversary</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Released in 2007, Anniversary is a reimagining of the 1996 original built within this new continuity. A young Lara is hired by Natla to recover the three pieces of the Scion of Atlantis, taking her from the mountains of Peru through Greece and Egypt to Atlantis. Though it came out after Legend, its events occur first in Lara's life in this universe. Natla's defeat here sets the stage for everything that follows.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/trlegend/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Legend cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Present day</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/trlegend/">Tomb Raider: Legend</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Years after her first adventure, Lara follows a trail of mysterious sword fragments across the globe — Bolivia, Tokyo, Ghana, Kazakhstan, Nepal, and Cornwall — each connected to a devastating event she witnessed as a child: the disappearance of her mother in a plane crash over Nepal. The investigation ultimately leads to revelations about Excalibur and the mythical realm of Avalon. Crystal Dynamics' debut with the franchise, released in 2006.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/trunderworld/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider: Underworld cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Shortly after Legend</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/trunderworld/">Tomb Raider: Underworld</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Directly continuing from Legend, Lara dives beneath the Mediterranean Sea in search of the Norse underworld of Niflheim and the truth about what happened to her mother. The investigation spans Thailand, the coast of Mexico, the Arctic, and the ruins of Croft Manor. The trilogy's conclusion resolves the mysteries of Lara's past and brings the Legend continuity to a close.</p>
  </div>

</div>

<h2 class="timeline-era-title">Survivor Trilogy</h2>
<p class="timeline-era-sub">Crystal Dynamics &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2013–2024</p>

<div class="timeline">

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr2013/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider (2013) cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">2013</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/tr2013/">Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Twenty-one-year-old Lara Croft joins a research expedition that is wrecked on the coast of Yamatai, a mythical island off Japan. Hunted by the Solarii Brotherhood — a cult under the control of the storm goddess Himiko's influence — Lara must push past every limit to survive and rescue her crew. Camilla Luddington voices Lara, and Rhianna Pratchett wrote the screenplay. This is the origin story of who Lara becomes.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/rottr/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Rise of the Tomb Raider cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Shortly after Yamatai</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/rottr/">Rise of the Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Still haunted by Yamatai, Lara becomes obsessed with proving the work of her late father — a disgraced archaeologist who believed in a divine source of immortality called the Divine Source. Her search takes her from Syria to the frozen Siberian wilderness, where she must reach the legendary lost city of Kitezh before Trinity, a powerful paramilitary organisation, claims its power for themselves.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/sottr/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Shadow of the Tomb Raider cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">Shortly after Kitezh</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/games/sottr/">Shadow of the Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--game">Game</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Lara steals a Mayan dagger from a Trinity site in Mexico, unknowingly triggering a cataclysmic series of natural disasters — a Mayan apocalypse the cult had been engineering for years. To stop it, she must venture deep into the Peruvian jungle and find Paititi, a hidden city that holds the key to averting what she started. Developed by Eidos Montréal, the Survivor trilogy ends with Lara confronting the cost of her relentless pursuit of the past.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/trlolc_tv/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="The Legend of Lara Croft cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">After Shadow</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/legend-of-lara-croft/">The Legend of Lara Croft</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--series">Series</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">This Netflix animated series picks up after the events of Shadow of the Tomb Raider. Lara investigates the theft of a powerful ancient artefact from a secret vault, a trail that takes her across Europe as she races against those who would use it to destabilise governments. Produced by Powerhouse Animation, it maintains full continuity with the Survivor trilogy games, with Hayley Atwell voicing Lara.</p>
  </div>

<h2 class="timeline-era-title">Film Continuities</h2>
<p class="timeline-era-sub">Standalone — not connected to any game series</p>

<div class="timeline">

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/lctr_movie/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Lara Croft: Tomb Raider cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">2001</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/lara-croft-tomb-raider/">Lara Croft: Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--film">Film</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Angelina Jolie stars as Lara Croft in the first live-action film. A rare planetary alignment activates a mysterious clock hidden inside Croft Manor, setting Lara on a race against the secret society of the Illuminati for two halves of an ancient device that can control time. Directed by Simon West, the film grosses over $274 million worldwide — one of the most commercially successful video game adaptations of its era.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/lctrcol_movie/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Lara Croft Tomb Raider: The Cradle of Life cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">2003</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/cradle-of-life/">Lara Croft Tomb Raider: The Cradle of Life</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--film">Film</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Jolie returns as Lara in the sequel. After recovering a golden orb from a submerged Macedonian temple, Lara finds herself in a race to reach Pandora's Box — a biological weapon of catastrophic power — before a bioterrorist can use it to reshape the world. The adventure spans Greece, Shanghai, Kenya, and the mythical Cradle of Life. Directed by Jan de Bont, lower box office returns ended the Jolie film series.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/tr2018_movie/thumbs/cover.jpg" alt="Tomb Raider (2018) cover" class="timeline-cover" loading="lazy">
    <p class="timeline-year">2018</p>
    <p class="timeline-title"><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/media/tomb-raider-2018/">Tomb Raider</a> <span class="timeline-tag timeline-tag--film">Film</span></p>
    <p class="timeline-desc">Alicia Vikander plays a young Lara Croft in this cinematic reboot, loosely inspired by the 2013 game but its own separate continuity. Searching for her missing father, Lara winds up stranded on Yamatai island, where a Trinity operative is attempting to unleash the supernatural power of the Japanese queen Himiko. Directed by Roar Uthaug, the film received a generally more positive reception than the Jolie-era films.</p>
  </div>
