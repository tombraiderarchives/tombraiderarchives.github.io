Crystal Dynamics has laid off eight more employees, about three months before its next Tomb Raider game ships. The studio called the move a “difficult, but necessary business decision” in a statement to the press. Legacy of Atlantis, its remake of the original game, launches February 12, 2027 on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S and Switch 2.

PC Gamer counts this as at least the studio’s fifth round of layoffs in under two years. It arrives eight months after the previous round. Fan concern is easy to understand.

The cuts have come at a steady pace. In March 2026 the studio confirmed 20 layoffs across development and central operations. Thirty jobs went in November 2025, and there were further cuts in March and August 2025.

This latest round surfaced through LinkedIn posts by affected staff. One was a UI software engineer. Another was a developer building procedural tools meant to support the team over the next 18 months. That planned work stretched well past the February launch.

Players are waiting on two delayed games. Legacy of Atlantis slipped from a 2026 window to February 2027. Catalyst moved less than two months after that June delay. Embracer’s Q1 FY 2026/27 interim report expects Catalyst in FY 2027/28. It will be published externally and made by Crystal Dynamics with Flying Wild Hog and Eidos Montréal. Embracer’s investor presentation lists Catalyst for PC, Xbox Series X|S and PS5.

The money behind both projects is shifting. Embracer’s CEO has said Amazon’s pullback from game development affected Tomb Raider. Embracer’s Q2 earnings call flagged that the revenue it earns from building the two games with Amazon falls as those projects wind down ahead of launch. The same call put wishlists and pre-orders for Legacy of Atlantis ahead of targets.

Taken together, the figures are consistent with a studio trimming staff as paid development work tapers, even while demand for its next release looks healthy. For fans, the open question is Catalyst, which still has more than a year of development ahead.

Embracer’s next interim report covers October to December 2026. It is scheduled for February 11, 2027, one day before Legacy of Atlantis goes on sale.
