If you’re wondering what versions of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will be available at launch, then we’ve got you covered.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is a full remake built from the ground up of the original Tomb Raider that first launched on the PlayStation all the way back in 1996.

As for the upcoming remake, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will be co-developed by series veterans Crystal Dynamics and will be joined by Flying Wild Hog (Trek to Yomi, Evil West). The remake will be powered by Unreal Engine 5.

No matter which version of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis you pick up, you will be gifted with the pre-order bonus. This bonus DLC comes with the ‘Survivor Outfit’ inspired by the 2013 reboot. This outfit is an early unlock, so you will be able to acquire it at some point, even without the pre-order DLC.

Of course, you can pick up the standard edition digitally and physically at retail. This will come with no juicy extras, other than the pre-order bonus. But if all you are about is playing Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, this is all you’ll ever need.

There are two versions of the Deluxe Edition, one being digital and the other physical, and one is certainly better than the other in terms of content.

The ‘Parisian Fugitive Outfit’, which is inspired by 2003’s Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness (it’s an early unlock)

The physical Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis comes with a steelbook case to store your disc.

If you want something extra fancy, then it doesn't get more fancy than having a stunning statue of Lara Croft fighting a T-Rex, a recreation of the iconic moment in the game.
