Embarking on this grand adventure, Lara Croft reconnects with her origins in more ways than one. The initial training stages transport players to Cambodia in 1984, where a young Lara undergoes fundamental lessons under the guidance of her mentor, Werner Von Croy. This mentorship, central to the main plot, continues into the present day.



Devotees of the original Tomb Raider will rejoice as the game revisits ancient ruins and reintroduces puzzle-centric gameplay. The contemporary narrative unfolds within a temple dedicated to the Egyptian god Set. Lara, in her quest for an amulet, inadvertently releases the malevolent deity into the world. Now faced with the task of recapturing him, she must act swiftly to safeguard humanity from impending peril.
