A new Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis video has been released by Crystal Dynamics and Amazon Game Studios, giving fans a better look at how Lara Croft will traverse the game’s dangerous locales.

This new video, which is the fourth episode of an ongoing mini-doc series about the upcoming remake, features several members of developer Flying Wild Hog — as well as Alix Wilton Regan, who’s portraying Croft herself — discussing how Lara’s movements and jumps were designed in Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis. They go into detail about how they make the different animations and movement styles feel fitting for the iconic treasure hunter, as well as how the game will subtly guide players towards their destinations as they play.
