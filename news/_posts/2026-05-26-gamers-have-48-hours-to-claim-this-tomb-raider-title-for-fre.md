A collection of three iconic Tomb Raider games are currently available to download and keep for free – but gamers will have to act fast as the offer is only available for the next 48 hours.

The offer is available over at the Epic Games Store with the remastered versions of Tomb Raider 1-3 being given away for free as part of this month’s offer. There’s no catch - all you need to do is create a free Epic Games account and redeem the freebies from the store.

The offer expires on May 28 at 4pm, so make sure you claim the offer before it expires. Sadly, this is only useful for PC players, although Xbox gamers considering picking up the upcoming Project Helix device could do well with cliaming it, too.

They’re a great set of games to return to even if you played the originals, especially as they likely look and run how you imagined they did back in the 90s. At the same time, they’re also a great play for those who might have only interacted with Lara through her modern games by Crystal Dynamics.

2026 and 2027 are set to be big years for Lara Croft as we’re set to get two brand new games. One – Legacy of Atlantis – is a full on remake of the first game and is scheduled to release later this year.

Meanwhile, Tomb Raider Catalyst is a completely new entry in the series and looks to be something of a soft reboot for Croft. That one is set to hit PC and consoles later in 2027.
