After an all-too-long sabbatical, Lara Croft is making her return to gaming soon, with the Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis release date now confirmed.

We're celebrating 30 years of Lara, and as part of that, we're also getting a new TV show starring Sophie Turner in the lead role.

But you're here for the games, and we've got all the details on Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, including release date, pre-orders, editions, who plays Lara and more.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is currently scheduled to release on 12 February 2027 for PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S and Nintendo Switch 2.

Initially, the game had been slated for a 2026 release, but whether because of development issues or GTA 6 avoidance, a delay was confirmed during the June 2026 PlayStation State of Play.

Fans will be hoping that this is the last delay, but with a concrete release date on the cards now, it feels like this could be it!

Pre-ordering the game will net you the Lara Croft Survivor Outfit, and if you pre-order the Deluxe Edition, you'll also get 48-hour early access – more on those below.

There are three editions of Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, each of which comes with a different number of rewards.

Lara Croft will be played by Alix Wilton Regan in Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis.

Wilton Regan has a storied career in gaming, and you will almost certainly recognise some of her biggest roles.

She's been Samantha Traynor in Mass Effect 3, Alt Cunningham in Cyberpunk 2077, the female Inquisitor in both Dragon Age: Inquisition and Veilguard, Lea Florence Monad in Lies of P: Overture and many more.

She takes over from Camilla Luddington, who voiced Lara in the previous three Tomb Raider games.
