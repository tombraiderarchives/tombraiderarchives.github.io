This week, Down in Bermuda and Tomb Raider 1-3 Remastered are available for free on the Epic Games Store. This is the second in a wave of free “Mystery” games that are becoming available for free on the PC gaming storefront. These free “mystery” games are becoming available during Epic Games’ “MEGA SALE”, which will be active until June 11th.

Tomb Raider I-III Remastered launched on PC and consoles in 2024. The games include upgraded graphics and the option to switch to the game’s original “polygonal” look. The games also include new features like photo mode and challenge mode.

Typically, the Tomb Raider Classic Trilogy is available for £24.99. This makes this free promotional period very attractive for Tomb Raider fans. Down in Bermuda, a point and click puzzle game, is also available for free this week.

PC gamers have until May 28th at 4 PM BST to pick up these free PC games on the Epic Games Store. After that, two new free “mystery games” will be available for free.
