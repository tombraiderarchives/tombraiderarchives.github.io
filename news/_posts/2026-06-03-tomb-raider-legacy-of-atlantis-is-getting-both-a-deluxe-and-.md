It's now official: Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis will be released on PS5, Xbox Series S|X, and PC on February 12, 2027. A Switch 2 version will be available at a later date.

Additionally, Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog have unveiled the different editions. By pre-ordering the Standard Edition for €59.99, players will receive a survivor outfit inspired by the 2013 reboot.

As for the Deluxe Edition priced at €69.99, it includes two days of early access to play starting February 10, post-launch DLC, and the Parisian Fugitive outfit from The Angel of Darkness.
