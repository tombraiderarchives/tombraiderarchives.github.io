The game is Zombie Army 4: Dead War, developed by Rebellion and released in 2020. It's a sequel to the previously released Zombie Army Trilogy, a collection of games that use Rebellion's familiar Sniper Elite gameplay in the context of a zombie horror.

In the game, you take on the undead hordes of the Nazi army in a series of co-operative missions that be played between one and four players. Each campaign takes you through a series of missions which often require you to get from Point A to Point B, but with some objectives sprinkled throughout similar to Left 4 Dead.

Similar to the Sniper Elite series, players use long-range rifles to eliminate threats, with the game utilising the trademark slow motion and x-ray vision camera that Rebellion is known for. Dismemberment and deep customisation are key here, as you fit yourself with a killer loadout to take on the undead hordes.
