Limited Run Games has announced details of the physical Switch 2 release for Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition. There’s a standard release priced at $50 and a Collector’s Edition for $80. You can see the Collector’s Edition above, which packs in the following:

Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition is the cinematic action-adventure that forced Lara Croft to grow from an inexperienced young woman into a hardened survivor has been re-built for next-gen consoles, featuring an obsessively detailed Lara and a stunningly lifelike world. Lara must endure high-octane combat, customize her weapons and gear, and overcome grueling environments to survive her first adventure and uncover the island’s deadly secret.

Pre-orders for Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition go live on July 2nd, 2026 and you can take a closer look here.
