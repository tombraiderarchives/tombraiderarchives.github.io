Embracer has begun a fifth round of layoffs at Crystal Dynamics since buying the studio in 2022, while two Tomb Raider games remain in development.

The cuts, reported by Rock Paper Shotgun on 17 November after an initial account by Vertical Slice, came to light through LinkedIn posts from affected staff. Those include senior game designer Joshua Mitchell, senior environment technical artist Kevin Baker and UI software engineer Garin R. K. Richards, each of whom shared news of their redundancy. More employees may be caught up in the round, but neither Crystal Dynamics nor Embracer has commented, and no total headcount has been given.

By Rock Paper Shotgun's tally, this is the studio's fifth round of redundancies under Embracer ownership, following cuts in March 2025, August 2025, November 2025 and March 2026. Embracer, the Swedish games group, acquired the Bay Area studio in 2022 and has been restructuring across its businesses ever since. No reason for this round has been offered by either company.

The redundancies land while Crystal Dynamics is midway through development on two Tomb Raider projects, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis and Tomb Raider: Catalyst. Legacy of Atlantis, a reimagining of Lara Croft's debut adventure co-developed with Flying Wild Hog and published by Amazon Game Studios, is dated for 12 February 2027 on Steam at €59.99. The store listing for that game states that generative AI was used "to support some early exploration and temporary development content. Any AI-assisted assets were either replaced or refined by humans in order to maintain the creative and artistic vision of the development team."

Tomb Raider: Catalyst has no release date. Neither project's status has been addressed in any company statement about the layoffs.
