The sequel to the 2001 film. An earthquake off the coast of Greece uncovers a submerged temple, inside which Lara finds a glowing orb — a map to the mythical Cradle of Life, the resting place of Pandora's Box. The orb is stolen by mercenaries working for Jonathan Reiss, a bio-weapons dealer who intends to use Pandora's Box as the ultimate weapon.

Lara teams up with ex-lover Terry Sheridan to track Reiss across China and into Africa. The film features a motorcycle chase along the Great Wall of China and an underwater temple sequence. Angelina Jolie returns as Lara, with Gerard Butler as the roguish Sheridan.
