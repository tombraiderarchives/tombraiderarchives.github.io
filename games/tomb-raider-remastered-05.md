Tomb Raider V: Chronicles Remastered is part of the Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered collection by Aspyr Media. This remaster of the 2000 anthology-structured entry brings all four of Lara's episodes to modern platforms with fully updated visuals.

Chronicles departs from a traditional linear narrative, instead following a group of Lara's friends gathered at her memorial service after the events of The Last Revelation. Through their reminiscences, four separate stories of Lara's past adventures unfold across Rome, Russia, Ireland, and New York — each with a distinct setting and tone.

The remaster preserves all four episodes and includes the visual toggle and optional modern controls found throughout the collection.
