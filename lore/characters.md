        <h2 class="char-name">Lara Croft</h2>
        <p class="char-bio">Lara Croft is the iconic protagonist of the Tomb Raider franchise, created by Toby Gard at Core Design in 1993. Initially conceived as a Brazilian adventurer named Laura Cruz, the character was reworked into British aristocrat Lady Lara Croft to avoid cultural clichés. She debuted in <em>Tomb Raider</em> in October 1996 and has since appeared in nineteen video game titles, two major film franchises, animated series, and a wide range of comics and merchandise.</p>
        <p class="char-bio">Across the franchise's three distinct continuities, Lara is consistently portrayed as highly intelligent, physically exceptional, and driven by an obsessive passion for archaeology and discovery. In the classic and Legend-era games, she is a self-assured British aristocrat who has long since mastered her craft. The 2013 Survivor trilogy reimagines her as a young academic thrust into danger before emerging as a hardened survivor.</p>
