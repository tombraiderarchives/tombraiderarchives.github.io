[OSEN=Jang Woo-young Reporter] Girl band QWER (QWER) sings the opening song for a Japanese animation.

QWER (Chodan, Magenta, Hina, Siyeon) released the second music video teaser for ‘SHOW DOWN’, the opening song of the Japanese animation “Tomb Raider King,” on their official YouTube channel at 12 p.m. on July 7. The teaser captures QWER’s unyielding advance against a red threat, inspired by the original work’s protagonist.

Amid fast-paced production, a mysterious flock of crows engulfing the sky appears, with QWER delivering explosive energy through a powerful band performance never seen before.

‘SHOW DOWN’, set to be released on various music sites on July 8, is QWER’s debut animation OST. The song conveys a bold message—“In a hypocritical world, we charge forward without concern for others’ judgment”—through powerful band sounds. Following the opening song ‘SHOW DOWN’ for “Tomb Raider King,” QWER also participated in the ending song ‘To Be Continued’, demonstrating the high level of local interest.
