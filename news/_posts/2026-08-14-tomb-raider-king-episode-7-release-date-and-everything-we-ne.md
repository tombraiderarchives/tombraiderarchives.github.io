Tomb Raider King Episode 7 has been looked forward to by anime fans as the latest installment has one of the most explosive events unfold. We see how Joo-Heon managed to take on a God Relic and at the same time also managed to finish some business with the boss who had done him wrong in his previous life. With our main character taking some ruthless choices and collecting relics, the upcoming installment just might end up raising stakes further, which would lead to bigger conflicts.

In this week's installment, we see how the main character confronts with Epyptian relic. The situation escalated when the Crow made an appearance and warned others to release the human. Then, the Tomb of Ancient Egypt emerged, and we see Osiris, Anubis, and other gods come into this conflict.

For a second, Joo-Heon was forced into a trial, but he managed to turn the odds against the god. After he escaped and fought through an army of undead creatures, the protagonist eventually defeated Anubis. Interestingly, this allowed him to conduct a trial on his own terms, and in the end, he got to claim another god relic.

However, the biggest shock came at the end. Tae-Joon, Ryoga's former boss and the person responsible for his previous suffering, offered to work together. Ryoga did not hesitate. He just took his sword out and killed him, and now fans are wondering how the story is going to develop from now on.

Looking at how intensely the previous installment ended, fans could expect the following from Tomb Raider King Episode 7:

Joo-Heon's new relic could change everything: After defeating the Egyptian gods, Joo-Heon has once again increased his collection of powerful relics. Tomb Raider King Episode 7 could explore exactly what his newest acquisition can do and how he plans to use it. Since Ryoga has extensive knowledge of the future, he is unlikely to collect relics simply for their raw power.

The Crow's mystery may deepen: The Crow remains one of the biggest mysteries in the anime. Episode 6 revealed that it has turned against the gods and appears to be helping humans fight against the relics. There were also hints that the Crow may have the ability to consume relics. Tomb Raider King Episode 7 could provide more clues about its origins, although the anime may deliberately keep its true identity hidden for longer.

Joo-Heon could become an even bigger threat: Perhaps the most interesting prediction for Tomb Raider King Episode 7 is that Joo-Heon himself could become the target of other relic users. His actions are becoming increasingly aggressive, and defeating multiple gods is bound to attract attention.

In conclusion, Tomb Raider King Episode 7 could be another major step forward for Ryoga's increasingly ruthless journey.
