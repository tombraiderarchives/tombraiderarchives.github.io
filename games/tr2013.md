A bold reboot that reimagined Lara Croft's origin story. A twenty-one-year-old Lara is shipwrecked on the mythical island of Yamatai in the Devil's Sea, where she must survive against a cult called the Solarii Brotherhood who worship the ancient Sun Queen Himiko.

Written by Rhianna Pratchett, the game emphasised Lara's vulnerability and growth from frightened survivor to hardened fighter. Its cinematic presentation and focus on character drew comparisons to The Last of Us. One of the best-selling Tomb Raider titles, it launched the Survivor Era trilogy and introduced Lara to a new generation.
