Tomb Raider King Episode 6 is finally here, and this installment ended up being an intense story, as this series has shown us so far. In this episode, we see how Joo-Heon found himself fighting against some really powerful God Relics and also had to face some people from his past. By the end of this installment, we see that he makes one difficult decision that has the potential to change the course of the story.

In Tomb Raider King Episode 6, our main character fights the relic user who has been pursuing him for some time now. This man believes that he has an upper hand just because of a powerful relic, but he soon understands as to why Joo-Heon is one of the most exceptional Tomb Raiders. Turns out that the main character manages to steal this relic even before the situation escalated.

Additionally, Tomb Raider King Episode 6, that his ability to take another relic without even making the opponent realise what just happened, made it an incredibly entertaining scene to watch. However, the confrontation quickly takes a supernatural turn when the mysterious Crow appears. It is the same crow because of which Joo-Heon managed to get a second shot in life.

The Crow warns the relic users that they are making a serious mistake by refusing to let Joo-Heon go. Its warning soon becomes reality as an enormous Egyptian tomb manifests, completely changing the battlefield. Additionally, the appearance of the tomb introduces several powerful figures from Egyptian mythology, including Anubis and Osiris.

Anubis decides to put Joo-Heon on trial, forcing him to answer a series of questions before he can obtain the relic he wants. Joo-Heon initially believes his knowledge of the future will help him survive the trial. However, there is an important complication: he has already changed the timeline.

Because of his actions, events are no longer guaranteed to unfold exactly as they did in his previous life. This means that relying too heavily on his memories could actually work against him.

The undead sequence is easily one of the biggest highlights of Tomb Raider King Episode 6. The Egyptian gods believe they can simply continue spawning undead until Joo-Heon is exhausted. But Joo-Heon remains completely confident. He explains that the tomb allows him to use his abilities to their fullest before drawing his sword and charging directly into the undead army.

Rather than simply escaping the Egyptian tomb, Joo-Heon in Tomb Raider King Episode 6 forces Anubis to repeat the trial on his terms. He essentially bullies the Egyptian god into giving him the opportunity he wants. After defeating Anubis, Joo-Heon obtains his relic and receives several new abilities and titles. His resistance to death-related relics increases, while his Tomb Raider abilities also improve.

But the episode still has one massive surprise left. After the Egyptian tomb disappears, Tae-Joon approaches Joo-Heon with an unexpected offer. Joo-Heon, however, has absolutely no interest in negotiating. Without wasting time explaining his motivations or revealing his plans, he simply draws his sword and kills Tae-Joon.

In conclusion, Tomb Raider King Episode 6 manages to change Joo-Heon's position in the story. He has made some different choices in his second chance in life to ensure that he does not go through the same kind of suffering. With him trying to rewrite everything in the upcoming episodes, viewers will get a better understanding of how much he has actually altered the timeline.
