Aspyr Media has officially confirmed a follow-up to their acclaimed Tomb Raider I–III Remastered collection. The new package — **Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered** — will bring three more classic Core Design entries to modern platforms with rebuilt visuals and optional modern controls.

The collection covers:

- **Tomb Raider IV: The Last Revelation** (originally 1999) — Lara's journey through ancient Egypt, with a story that concludes in a cliffhanger that shocked fans at the time.
- **Tomb Raider V: Chronicles** (originally 2000) — an anthology-style game told through flashbacks, following Lara's friends as they recount her past adventures.
- **Tomb Raider VI: The Angel of Darkness** (originally 2003) — the controversial Paris-set entry that pushed Lara in a darker, story-heavy direction.

As with the previous collection, players will be able to toggle between classic and remastered visuals at any point during gameplay. Achievement and trophy support will be included across all platforms.

No firm release date has been announced beyond 2025. We will update this page when more details become available.
