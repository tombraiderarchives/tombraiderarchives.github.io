Shadow of the Tomb Raider is officially out now on Nintendo Switch 2. With this shadow drop, all three parts of the Tomb Raider Survivor Trilogy are now fully playable on Nintendo Switch 2.

Originally released in September 2018 for the PC, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One, Shadow of the Tomb Raider is the 12th mainline Tomb Raider entry, and the final installment in Eidos-Montreal's Tomb Raider Survivor Trilogy. Upon its debut, Shadow of the Tomb Raider earned a positive reception from critics, with the action-adventure game earning a Top Critic Average of 80 on OpenCritic, and a critic recommendation score of 68%. In GameRant's Shadow of the Tomb Raider review, Dalton Cooper gave the game an 8/10, praising its narrative, environments, and puzzles, but criticizing the game's lack of replayability.

The first Black Friday deals for Nintendo Switch 2 games are live now, ahead of the official holiday.

Shadow of the Tomb Raider has just been shadow-dropped on Nintendo Switch 2. Like its Switch 2 predecessors, this Definitive Edition of Shadow of the Tomb Raider comes with all of the game's base content and DLC, including seven challenge tombs, 2-player co-op, and a range of cosmetics....
