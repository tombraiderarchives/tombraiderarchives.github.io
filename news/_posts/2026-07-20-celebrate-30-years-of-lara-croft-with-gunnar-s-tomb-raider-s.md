This year marks 30 years of the beloved video game franchise Tomb Raider! To celebrate, Crystal Dynamics is partnering with Gunnar on a pair of Tomb Raider Croft Glasses, inspired by the iconic heroine Lara Croft.

Gunnar's Tomb Raider glasses come with themed accessories for the skilled adventurer. | Source: Gunnar

Gunnar's Tomb Raider glasses come with one of three different lens styles. | Source: Gunnar

The Tomb Raider Croft Glasses are available now at gear.tombraider.com.

Croft glasses with the Amber Max tint, alongside the accessories. | Source: Gunnar Optiks

The Croft Glasses are available in an 'Amber' tint. | Source: Gunnar Optiks

Tomb Raider fans can get the Croft Glasses with a 'Sun' tint.
