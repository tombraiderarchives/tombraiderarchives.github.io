Crystal Dynamics continues its work on the Tomb Raider remake, aiming to preserve the iconic game’s atmosphere while adapting it to modern standards. The developers have released a video showcasing the changes coming to puzzles, traps, and exploration in Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is an ambitious project serving as a full-fledged remake of the legendary 1996 title. The studio is developing it with support from Polish colleagues at Flying Wild Hog. The team’s primary goal is to bridge the gap between nostalgia and modern mechanics while keeping the original spirit intact.

Complex puzzles were a signature feature of the original game, and the remake aims to deepen that experience. The developers are reworking classic puzzle solutions, ensuring that veteran players cannot rely on muscle memory alone. The logic of ancient mechanisms has been redesigned, demanding critical thinking from the player.
