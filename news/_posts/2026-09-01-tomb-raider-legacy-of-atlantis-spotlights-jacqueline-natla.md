Amazon Game Studios and Crystal Dynamics have released the seventh Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis mini-documentary, this time focused on Jacqueline Natla.

The episode explores Lara Croft’s nemesis: the immortal former ruler of Ancient Atlantis and CEO of Natla Technologies.

Jacqueline Natla will oppose Lara Croft as she searches for Atlantis’s secrets.

As one of the key figures from Lara’s 1996 debut adventure, Natla fills the role of a powerful rival who keeps pushing Lara further into danger.

Adriene Wilkinson will play Natla in Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis.

In the new mini-doc, Wilkinson describes Natla as a “noble foe” who raises the stakes for Lara every time she acts. She also frames Natla as the kind of villain who pushes the hero harder, making the conflict feel personal and dangerous.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis reimagines Lara Croft’s original 1996 adventure for modern platforms.

The mini-documentary series has focused on different parts of the remake’s development, with this latest episode turning its attention to one of the series’ most recognizable villains.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis launches February 12, 2027, for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam.
