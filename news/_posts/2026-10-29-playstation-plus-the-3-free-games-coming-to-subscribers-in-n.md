PlayStation Plus has announced its monthly games lineup for November, available to play from 3 November until 30 November.

The titles featured in November's selection are Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV – VI Remastered, and Monster Train 2.

Dead Island 2 provides a visceral zombie-slaying action RPG set in Los Angeles, featuring both single-player and three-player co-op modes.

Tomb Raider IV – VI Remastered includes Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation, Tomb Raider: Chronicles and Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness, while Monster Train 2 sees you command clans formed of a makeshift alliance between former angels and demons.

PlayStation Plus members have until Monday 2 November to claim October’s games, which include F1 25, Hunt: Showdown 1896, and Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2.
