A faithful remake of the original 1996 Tomb Raider built on the Legend engine, created to celebrate the franchise's 10th anniversary. Anniversary retells Lara's first adventure across Peru, Greece, Egypt, and Atlantis with fully rebuilt environments and modernised controls.

The game stays true to the spirit and structure of the original while making it accessible to players new to the series. Crystal Dynamics brought their distinctive fluid movement system and refined the classic puzzles for a new generation.
