Sony will add Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered and Monster Train 2 to the PlayStation Plus Monthly Games line-up on Tuesday 3 November 2026, according to a post on the PlayStation.Blog published on 28 October 2026.

All three games can be added to a member’s library until Monday 30 November 2026.

Dead Island 2 and Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered are listed for PS5 and PS4, while Monster Train 2 is listed for PS5 only. The post was written by Adam Michel, senior director of content acquisition and operations at Sony Interactive Entertainment.

Dead Island 2 is set in Los Angeles after a zombie outbreak, in a version of the city the post calls “HELL-A”. Sony describes it as a role-playing game built around melee combat, with quests to take on and survivors to meet along the way.

Players can go through the game alone or in online three-player co-op.

Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered collects three games Sony calls the Darkness Trilogy.

Those are Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation, Tomb Raider: Chronicles and Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness. “Play with the original polygonal models or swap to the remastered graphics at any time,” Michel wrote in the post.

Monster Train 2 is a single-player deckbuilding roguelite. Sony says players command clans made up of former angels and demons who have joined forces against beings called Titans, who have taken control of Heaven. The game moves through Hell, Heaven and the Abyss, with five clans to choose from.

Each clan has its own strengths and style of play, according to the post.

Players must defend three vertical levels against enemies, and the post says they will need to recruit units, upgrade cards and experiment with different approaches to survive. Monster Train 2 is the only game of the three that is not available on PS4.

PlayStation Plus members have until Monday 2 November 2026 to add October’s games.

Those are F1 25, Hunt: Showdown 1896 and Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2. The post describes 2 November as the last chance to add them, and any of the three claimed by that date go into a member’s library.

The two line-ups follow on consecutive days. October’s games stop being available to add on Monday 2 November 2026, and the November games become available to add on Tuesday 3 November 2026.
