FuturLab has announced that its Final Fantasy VII and Tomb Raider DLC packs for cleaning sim PowerWash Simulator will be delisted soon, although you'll still be able to play them if you already have them.

This news was revealed via the official FuturLab website, where the developer says that on May 19th, both DLC packs will disappear from all storefronts and won't be available to claim anymore. If you've already claimed them, though, you can redownload them as normal.

According to FuturLab, the removal of both DLC packs also coincides with "the end of [the studio's] formal relationship with [original PowerWash Simulator publisher] Square Enix". The developer describes the publisher as "an incredible partner".

PowerWash Simulator's Tomb Raider DLC pack was released in January 2023, bringing five levels based on iconic locations from the adventure series to spray down.

The Final Fantasy VII DLC pack followed in March that same year, introducing the likes of Tifa's Seventh Heaven bar and the (not so) intimidating Guard Scorpion to clean, along with three other themed stages.
