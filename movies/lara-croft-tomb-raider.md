The film that brought Lara Croft to cinema screens worldwide. Lara discovers a mysterious ticking clock hidden inside Croft Manor — a clock with no hands. A letter from her late father leads her to the Illuminati's plot to retrieve the All-Seeing Eye, an artefact capable of controlling time itself.

Lara must race against the Illuminati, led by Manfred Powell, to recover both halves of the Eye before a rare planetary alignment gives the artefact its full power. Angelina Jolie's committed physical performance and globe-trotting locations — Cambodia's Angkor Wat, Venice, Iceland — made it a box-office hit and one of the most successful video game adaptations of its era.
