Tomb Raider’s 30th anniversary takes center stage in a new mini-documentary from Amazon Game Studios and Crystal Dynamics. The latest episode in the Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis documentary series visits October’s TR30 fan event in Derby, England, bringing together fans and people involved in Lara Croft’s history. TryNew Games

The October celebration gathered hundreds of attendees in the city where Tomb Raider began. Guests included original developers, Lara Croft voice actors and models, alongside members of the teams working on Legacy of Atlantis.

The documentary focuses on the connections between those groups. It examines what the series means to its creators and performers, as well as the community that has supported Lara across three decades.

Rather than concentrating on a single game, the episode uses the anniversary gathering to consider the franchise’s history through the people attending. That includes the fans who have continued celebrating the character long after their first encounter with her.

Alison Carroll, who portrayed Lara Croft as an official model from 2008–2010, credits fans with sustaining the franchise.

“Without the fans, Tomb Raider wouldn’t still be alive today,” Carroll says, pointing to the 400 people attending the celebration as evidence of that support.

Alix Wilton Regan, Lara Croft’s voice actress, also discusses the character’s lasting appeal. Her comments focus on the qualities that make Lara someone fans aspire to be, summed up in her observation: “We all want to be Lara Croft.”

Together, those perspectives connect the event’s celebration of the past with the character who remains at the center of the series.

The announcement centers on the documentary and fan event, rather than providing new gameplay details or release information. For now, the video offers a look at the community surrounding the upcoming game and the history its team is building upon.
