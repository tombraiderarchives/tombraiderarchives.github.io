Aspyr has decided to port Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration to Switch 2. Best thing is, you don't have to wait to play it, as the port is available now.

The game is currently on sale in the eShop for €26.99 thanks to a 10% launch discount (valid through June 22). All DLC and updates are included in this edition.
