Amazon’s Tomb Raider page now lists Switch 2 only for Legacy of Atlantis, following earlier wording that included both upcoming games.

Amazon has changed its official Tomb Raider webpage to remove references to a Nintendo Switch 2 version of Tomb Raider: Catalyst, alongside wording placing its release in 2028.

The changes follow reports that the publisher’s website had confirmed both details. Its current article lists PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Steam for both upcoming Tomb Raider games, while naming Switch 2 specifically for Legacy of Atlantis.

An earlier version of Amazon’s announcement, distributed through PublicNow on November 9, included Nintendo’s console in a shared platform list. It also explicitly placed Catalyst’s launch in 2028. That version remains indexed with the earlier wording.

The difference is clearest in the section covering release dates and platforms.

The distributed version stated that Legacy of Atlantis would arrive on February 12, 2027, followed by Catalyst in 2028. It then listed PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and Steam as platforms for both titles.

On Amazon’s current website, Catalyst instead has no announced release date, and the Switch 2 reference applies only to Legacy of Atlantis. The page still displays November 9 as its update date, without explaining the revised wording.

Legacy of Atlantis remains listed for February 12, 2027, including on Switch 2. It rebuilds Lara Croft’s original adventure, while Catalyst takes her to Northern India later in her story.
