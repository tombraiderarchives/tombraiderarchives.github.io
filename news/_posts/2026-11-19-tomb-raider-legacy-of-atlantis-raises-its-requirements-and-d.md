Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, the return of Lara Croft developed by the Polish studio Flying Wild Hog in collaboration with Crystal Dynamics, will arrive on PC on February 12, 2027. The title has updated its requirements on Steam and now requires Windows 11 and DirectX 12 as a mandatory baseline to be able to run on computers by that year.

The update on Steam details that for 1080p scaled on low at 30 FPS, a GeForce RTX 2070 or a Radeon RX 5700 is required along with 16 GB of RAM. For high at 60 FPS the bar rises to an RTX 3080 or RX 6800 XT, always with upscaling enabled and an 80 GB SSD. The game does without Denuvo, a detail that those who closely follow PC performance will appreciate.

Lara Croft has been raiding tombs for years, but now it seems she also wants to raid the player's pocket. If your rig sports a graphics card from a few years ago, get ready to look at the market with nostalgia. That said, without Denuvo, so at least the suffering will be only financial and not performance-related.
