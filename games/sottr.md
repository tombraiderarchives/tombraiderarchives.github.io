The conclusion of the Survivor Era trilogy, Shadow takes Lara to Mexico and Peru in pursuit of Trinity. After accidentally triggering a Mayan apocalypse, she must travel to the hidden city of Paititi to prevent the end of the world — while confronting the consequences of her increasingly reckless decisions.

Developed by Eidos Montréal rather than Crystal Dynamics, Shadow emphasised stealth and jungle environments, with Lara blending into the landscape more than ever before. The game is praised for its lush environments and the optional tombs, which are considered some of the best in the series.
