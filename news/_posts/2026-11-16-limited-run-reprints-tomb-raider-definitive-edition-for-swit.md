Limited Run Games has confirmed a second print run of the physical edition of Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition for Nintendo Switch 2. The first one sold out and the distributor launched a survey among its customers: 90 percent asked for more copies. The other installments of the Survivor trilogy are still available on its website. The news arrives in the middle of the saga's 30th anniversary and coincides with the surprise release of Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition on the Switch 2 eShop.

Limited Run's decision responds to a specific supply problem: the first batch sold out before many collectors could complete their order. With this second print run, the distributor adjusts production to the real demand measured in its survey. The physical format maintains its purpose for those who avoid digital purchases and for those looking to keep the complete Survivor trilogy on cartridge. The order window will open in the coming weeks and the details of the deadline and shipping will be published on the Limited Run website.

It turns out that asking customers if they want more copies of a sold-out product has a fairly predictable answer. 90 percent said yes, and the other 10 percent probably already had their unit reserved. Limited Run has turned a single-question survey into a business plan, and you have to give it credit: logistics works better when you ask before manufacturing. Now all that's missing is a survey to find out if collectors want print runs not to sell out in the first place.
