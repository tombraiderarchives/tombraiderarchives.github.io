It's the spookiest night of the year - and while footballers are gearing up for their upcoming clashes over the weekend, their WAGs might be looking ahead to their outfits for Saturday night. And they certainly have some stiff competition.

In the past few years we've seen jaw-dropping WAGs flaunt their looks in couples costumes, bridezilla's and even Lara Croft. And before our social media is flooded with snaps with parties, nightclubs and spooky houses, Daily Star Sport has rounded up some of the best outfits.

So without any further adu, here are seven of the best WAG Halloween outfits from recent years...

Ilkay Gundogan has had plenty of partners in crime in the middle of the park from Borussia Dortmund to Barcelona and Manchester City. However, his biggest partner in crime might be his jaw-dropping WAG Sara.

And, back in 2023, the loved-up duo turned evil for the spookiest night of the year as they dressed up as the joker and Harley Quinn. However, the streets of Gotham are nothing compared to Manchester's food scene for Sara.

Speaking about restaurants when the pair lived in Manchester, she said: "Sorry I'm sad to be honest but nothing. I tried so bad to find a good restaurant but...horrible food everywhere!"

Sophie Leno wowed as a "bridezilla"(Image: sophiechristin_/Instagram)

Zombie bride, bridezilla, whatever you want to call the costume, Sophie Leno delighted her Instagram followers with the costume that also featured fake blood.

Like the keeper, Sophie hails from Germany and the pair married in 2020 after spending five years together.

Leno is still playing on these shores, and remains at Fulham after moving from Arsenal in 2022. And in eight Premier League appearances this season he has kept three clean sheets.

Sergio Ramos and Pilar Rubio love to go all out for Halloween(Image: sergioramos/Instagram)

As one of football's current power couple, Sergio Ramos and Pilar Rubio went the full monty back in 2024 with their outfit, as the pair dressed as killer clowns.

R amos donned a full face of paint, and his long hair was styled upwards like Pennywise from Stephen King's IT. Pilar, who posed next to him, had her hair in two buns - and she wore some red and black stockings with a short leather top.

Ramos might have some time on his hands this year to plan another outfit, having last played professional football in December 2025 for Mexican side Monterrey.

Robyn Emma Keen showed off her jaw-dropping looks(Image: robynemmakeen/Instagram)

We're back to bridezillas with Jack Hendry's stunning girlfriend Robyn Keen, a BooHoo ambassador from the West Midlands. Robyn has been dubbed as the 'Britain's most beautiful WAG' and it's easy to see why.

She has brains to add to her beauty - and she studied a psychology degree at the University of Nottingham, close to where she was born in the West Midlands. However, it was in 2018 when she met the then-Celtic star.

She has since followed her beau to the continent - when he turned out for Club Brugge in Belgium - and then to Saudi Arabia, where he plays for Al-Ettifaq.

Alessia Elefante and Gianluigi Donnarumma often win Halloween with their outfits

City's No.1 is in safe hands with the jaw-dropping brunette Alessia Elefante, the pair are childhood sweethearts.

The Naples beauty once revealed she's a big fan of shopping, with Gucci being her favourite designer. She is also an interior designer. However, her true calling might just be Halloween costumes.

The Italian shot-stopper was forced to play second fiddle to the stunning Alessia as the pair looked like extras from Pirates of the Caribbean.

Aine May Kennedy left little to the imagination as she spilled out of her red latex outfit. Her beau, Conor Gallagher, snuck under the radar as he posed as the Big Bad Wolf in a mask.
