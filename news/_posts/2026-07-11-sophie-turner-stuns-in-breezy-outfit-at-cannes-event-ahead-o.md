Sophie Turner celebrated the opening of St-Germain Club Riviera at Mondrian Cannes in a Riviera-inspired outfit by Tae Park, complete with a St-Germain [Hugo] Spritz in hand and Gianvito Rossi wedges.

Turner's appearance marked her latest collaboration with St-Germain, the elderflower liqueur brand, at the newly launched seasonal spritz destination on Cannes’ La Croisette.

While details about Turner's upcoming Tomb Raider series remain scarce, the actress playing Lara Croft in the new video game, Alix Wilton Regan, expressed confidence in the show's quality due to Turner and show creator Phoebe Waller-Bridge's involvement.

From the Stark family to Lara Croft, Sophie Turner sure knows how to pick 'em when it comes to hit film and TV franchises.

And while fans wait for her upcoming Tomb Raider series to drop on Prime Video — expected next year — they can check out the stunning actress, 30, in new photos from a glamorous event in France.

On Saturday, July 11, Turner celebrated the opening of St-Germain Club Riviera at Mondrian Cannes. For the occasion, the Game of Thrones star wore a breezy Riviera-inspired set by Tae Park, featuring a sky-blue chiffon top and matching flowy chiffon skirt with soft matcha trim — a fresh, summery take on Côte d'Azur dressing for an evening in the gardens of Mondrian Cannes.

The appearance marked Turner's latest moment with St-Germain, the elderflower liqueur brand known for the hit summer cocktail of the summer — the St-Germain [Hugo] Spritz. She took a break from her busy summer filming schedule to celebrate the opening of the St-Germain Club Riviera, the newly launched seasonal spritz destination on Cannes' world-famous La Croisette, billed as the biggest Spritz Club in Cannes.

And on the topic of "filming," not a whole lot has been revealed about Turner's upcoming Tomb Raider series. But the actress playing Lara Croft in the new video game, Alix Wilton Regan, recently told Variety that the show is "going to be fantastic, because anything that [Turner and show creator Phoebe Waller-Bridge] do is really, really good."
