The Crew Motorfest just launched on Nintendo Switch 2, and that includes the game’s Tomb Raider: Secrets of Niʻihau DLC. Now Ubisoft has shared a new trailer showing off how Lara Croft looks driving away from a massive T-Rex. You can check it out above.

Launching alongside Season 11 on October 7, 2026, Tomb Raider: Secrets of Niʻihau is a premium adventure bringing together a brand-new Playlist, a mysterious new island to explore, and exclusive vehicles inspired by this legendary franchise.

Players will set foot on Niʻihau, an island that was previously inaccessible in The Crew Motorfest. While the island will be visible on the map for all players, access to its environment and activities will be reserved for owners of the Year 4 Pass or the standalone Tomb Raider DLC pack.

In this new adventure, players will follow Lara Croft’s guidance to help her find her missing friend Jonah and uncover an ancient relic that could change the course of history.

Designed in collaboration with Crystal Dynamics, Tomb Raider: Secrets of Niʻihau introduces a new style of gameplay focused on exploration, enigmas, and puzzle-solving. Players will need to use their surroundings and the new winch feature to overcome environmental challenges, explore ancient ruins, and progress through the island.

Separated from the other islands for centuries, Niʻihau has preserved secrets long forgotten by the rest of the world, including the presence of prehistoric creatures. During their journey, players will encounter dinosaurs roaming the island, from a massive Mosasaurus to a thrilling chase involving a T-Rex.

The Tomb Raider Secrets of Ni’ihau also expands the vehicle collection with 5 exclusive vehicles, giving players new ways to experience this unique adventure and continue their journey across Motorfest.

Tomb Raider: Secrets of Niʻihau will be available as part of the Year 4 Pass or as a standalone purchase on October 7th.
