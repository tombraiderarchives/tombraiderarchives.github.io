Today, Aspyr has announced a new update for the Tomb Raider I-III Remastered collection. These are all features that the community has asked them to add, along with some bug fixes and optimisations.

The three big new changes include a brightness adjustment, as the newer graphics style was just a bit of a departure from the lighting of the original. Classic Mode now runs at 60 FPS, and items you can pick up are now highlighted. They were really difficult to spot in some instances.

A whole bunch of bug fixes are also detailed, including some that are specific to the Switch 2 version.
