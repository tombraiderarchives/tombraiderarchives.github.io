Limited Run Games, a premium publisher of physical video games, in partnership with developer Aspyr® announced today that Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition is coming physically to the Nintendo® Switch™ 2. Fans and physical collectors will be able to pre-order both Standard and Collector’s Editions exclusively through Limited Run Games starting November 6, 2026.

Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition is the award-winning action-adventure experience and the harrowing final chapter of the Survivor Trilogy. As you race to save the world from a Mayan apocalypse, you’ll explore exotic locations, including the legendary Incan city of Paititi, while braving the unforgiving jungle and deadly tombs. Use advanced stealth techniques and an arsenal of weapons to stop the shadowy organization Trinity from stealing the power of an ancient god before it’s too late.

The Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition packages the complete experience together, including the base game, all seven DLC challenge tombs, plus all downloadable side missions, weapons, and all downloadable outfits, and skills. Also included in the Standard Edition is a free slipcover that allows you to keep your Tomb Raider trilogy – Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration, and Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition – together in one place.

Physical pre-orders launch on November 6, 2026, at 12:00 PM ET, and will remain open through December 27, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET.
