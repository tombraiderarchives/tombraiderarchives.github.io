The Tomb Raider King animated series has gotten its second trailer and second main visual ahead of its July 8, 25:15 JST (effectively July 9, 01:15 JST) Japanese broadcast start date. It was also announced the ending song for the South Korean animation is “To Be Continued” by the band QWER, who is also the opening song artist.

Tomb Raider King is an adaptation of the webtoon drawn by 3B2S. Yen Press publishes volumes of the comic in English and describes the synopsis as such:

In the year 2025, mysterious tombs appeared all over the world, each housing an ancient relic granting supernatural abilities. The lucky few who quickly obtained these relics ruled over powerful monopolies. Others came to serve them, such as Jooheon Suh, a “raider” who hid in tombs to steal relics. Betrayed by his employer, Jooheon is left to die but instead finds himself transported 15 years in the past. Armed with years of tough raiding experience and knowledge of what is to come in the world of relics, does Jooheon have what it takes to become the “Tomb Raider King”?

The Japanese dub cast includes Yoshimasa Hosoya as Goriki Ryoga (Jooheon Suh), Saori Hayami as Irene Holton, and Junichi Suwabe as Taisei Ogawara (Tae joon Kwon). Woo Seung Wook is directing the animation at STUDIO EEK and is also the series composer. Lee Hyun Joung is the character designer.

The Tomb Raider King webtoon is based on the web novel by SAN.G, and has REDICE STUDIO’s Yuns credited for the adaptation task. In Japan, the comic is serialized in Japan on Piccoma, with KADOKAWA publishing volumes under the MF Comics label.
