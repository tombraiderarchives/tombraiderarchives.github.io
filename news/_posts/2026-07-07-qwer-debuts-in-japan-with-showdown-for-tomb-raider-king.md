[OSEN=Sun Mi-kyung, reporter] Band QWER has taken its first step into the Japanese market with its intense and energetic charm.

QWER announced on July 8 the opening song ‘Showdown (SHOW DOWN)’ for the Japanese animation “Tomb Raider King,” officially declaring its local debut.

This song well captures QWER’s new charm. Unlike previous works, it embodies an energetic charm through intense and powerful band sounds. It contains a bold message of “breaking through without being conscious of others’ gaze.”

Through this opening song ‘Showdown (SHOW DOWN)’ for “Tomb Raider King,” QWER is conveying its unique new charm and message, officially announcing its global activities.
